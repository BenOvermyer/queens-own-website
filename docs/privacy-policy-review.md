# Privacy policy draft review

The standalone policy at `/privacy-policy/` replaces the contradictory policy from the original homepage. Its front matter marks it as a draft; it has no adoption or effective date. Review and approve the operating commitments before treating it as an adopted policy.

Confirm these points with the club coordinators:

- The responsible organization, jurisdiction, and listed privacy contacts, including who will receive and handle requests.
- Membership and editorial workflows, particularly explicit permission for publication of identifying details. Older handouts and guidance may need corresponding revisions.
- The no-sale and no-marketing-list-sharing statement, editor access controls, and actual handling of withdrawal and removal requests.
- All hosting, file-delivery, email, backup, and analytics services, their settings, retention periods, and any contractual safeguards. Server-side analytics cannot be ruled out by inspecting repository code.
- Retention and deletion procedures, response timing, and whether additional rights disclosures are required by applicable law.
- Whether children participate, their ages, and the consent procedures and legal requirements that apply. This draft does not establish an age-verification or parental-consent process.

Repository inspection found no website forms, embedded Facebook widgets, or client-side analytics scripts in the built pages, but did find externally hosted images in older content. The policy therefore avoids claiming that browsing is anonymous or that no third parties receive data. It also avoids absolute confidentiality promises and an unqualified disclaimer of responsibility for member posts.

Drafting references: the [FTC’s privacy and security guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/privacy-security) explains that privacy promises must be honored. Its [personal-information guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/protecting-personal-information-guide-business) supports limiting collection and retention. [Netlify’s analytics documentation](https://docs.netlify.com/manage/monitoring/web-analytics/how-web-analytics-works/) explains server-side request-based analytics, including IP-based visitor counts. These sources inform the draft; they do not establish which laws apply to this club or which provider features are enabled.
