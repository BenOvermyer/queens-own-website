</p><h2>Children of Velgarth</h2>
<p>
This fanzine is published periodically when it reaches 80 pages or so.  Zines are only available by e-mail and are completely free. The zines are in PDF format, which use Adobe Reader available for free from the Adobe website. Watch the Queen's Own newsletter for release dates.
</p><p>
Issues of <i>Children of Velgarth</i> will be available as long as Queen's Own is in business. You can order any issue at any time. When you send your request, please include a note stating which issue you want.  Send your e-mail request to either Healer Adept Moonstar at <a href="mailto:healermoon@gmail.com">healermoon@gmail.com</a> or
Herald-Mage Adept Danya Winterborn at <a href="mailto:shashtah@gmail.com">shashtah@gmail.com</a>.
</p><p>
</p><center><img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/barcomp.gif"/></center>
<p>
</p><h2>Submission Information:</h2>
<p>
This fanzine accepts:
</p><p>
</p><ul>
<li>All current personae (May 2000 and later) WILL be included unless 
requested otherwise.
</li><li>Past personae materials accepted by Queen's Own must include: 
    <ul>
<li>Month/year accepted
    </li><li>Gifts/Bondbird/etc. your persona may have
    </li><li>Your e-mail address
    </li><li>State and/or country in which you live
    </li><li>If it is your first personae or an NPC that you have developed.
    </li></ul>
</li></ul>
<p>
This fanzine does <font color="red">not</font> contain any subsequent stories, poetry or artwork.  <font color="red">Please</font> send these to the other QO-approved <a href="/qofanfic.htm">fanzines</a>.
</p><p>
Persona sketches, poems and artwork do not require release forms.  Personae presented in story-form do.  In other words, if you are simply talking about your persona, that's fine.  But if you are telling a story about your persona, then you need to file a release.
</p><p>
If you want, you may submit personae materials via e-mail when possible.  Send persona sketches, stories, poems and other written text in either MS Word or .txt/ASCII format.  You can scan pictures, costume designs, and so forth, and send them either as .jpgs or .gifs.  If you do not have a scanner, you can still submit your text by e-mail and snail mail any pictures or other nontext items.  If you do not have your text in electronic format, you can snail mail that as well.  E-mail submissions for the fanzine to <a href="mailto:healermoon@gmail.com">Healer Adept Moonstar</a>.
</p><p>
Please contact Healer Adept Moonstar at <a href="mailto:healermoon@gmail.com">healermoon@gmail.com</a> if you have any questions.
</p><p>
Thank you very much and good luck to all.
</p><p>
