</p><h2>Basic Requirements</h2>
<p>
</p><h2>I. Develop a Character</h2>
<p>
Develop a character (your persona--Who would you be if you could daydream yourself into 
Valdemar?  Be the "real" you or as different as you please.)  
and write about him/her.  Prepare a minimum of 3 pages.
</p><p>
This is a biography.  Describe your persona's physical and emotional 
characteristics, likes and dislikes, whatever makes you uniquely you.  
You may use first or third person.  
</p><p>
Remember, write about your persona <font color="red">before</font> s/he became a sorcerer/sorceress.  A sorcerer/sorceress must learn to cope with his/her magic, not have his/her life built around it.  Mage Gift usually manifests during puberty; it is not evident
at birth.  (10 points.)
</p><p>
</p><center><img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/barstar.gif"/></center>
<p>
</p><h2>II.  Develop One or More Friend or Relative Characters</h2>
<p>
You needn't be quite so detailed in your descriptions of these other characters.  But remember, you are writing about people you know well.  Describe what your family and/or friends look like, their own character traits, etc.  Again, a minimum of 3 pages.  (5 points)
</p><p>
</p><center><img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/barstar.gif"/></center>
<p>
</p><h2>III.  Extra Point Requirements (5 points each)</h2>
<p>
Choose and complete the requirements for two (minimum) of the following:
</p><p>
</p><ol>
<li>Write a story of more than 3 pages.
</li><li>Design a costume. 
</li><li>Create a costume. 
</li><li>Draw a picture of your character.
</li><li>Draw a picture of your "familiar".
</li><li>Illustrate your story.
</li><li>Write a chant.
</li><li>Compose a song, music and/or write lyrics.
</li><li>Enroll in a Yoga class.
</li><li>Draw a map. 
</li><li>Learn CPR and basic first-aid.
</li><li>Become a regular blood donor. 
</li><li>Become a volunteer. 
</li><li>Learn the uses of herbs in home remedies.
</li><li>Start a local <a href="/qochap.htm">chapter of Queen's Own</a>.
</li><li>Learn to ride a horse. 
</li><li>Learn the care and feeding of horses.
</li><li>Send a story, poem, or art to one of our <a href="/qofanfic.htm">zines</a>.
</li></ol>

</p><h2>Advanced Rank</h2>
<p>
There are several ways you can advance in rank as a mage.
</p><p>
<a id="witch" name="witch"></a>
</p><h3>Specialties</h3>
<p>
To earn one of these specialties, first create an approved Sorcerer/Sorceress persona.  Then complete the requirements next to the specialty that you want:
</p><p>
</p><ul>
<li>Earth-Witch: Write a 20-page paper on Earth Magic on Velgarth.
</li><li>Weatherwitch: Write a 20-page paper on the uses of Weather Magic on Velgarth.
</li></ul>
<p>
</p><h3>Formal Training</h3>
<p>
If you want your character to receive formal training in the magical arts, there are three schools that I know about.  Choose which school you want to join.  Complete the requirements in that column for each rank.  Do not switch columns.  If you start in the White Winds column, you need to stay there.
