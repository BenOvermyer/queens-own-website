</p><h2>Basic Requirements</h2>
<p>
</p><h2>I.  Clan and Position</h2>
<p>
Honor applies; what you roll is what you get.
</p><p>
</p><h3>Clan</h3>
<p>
Determine your Clan by rolling two six-sided dice or one die twice and add the
numbers.
</p><p>
</p><table border="0">
<tr>
<td>2-3:</td>
<td>Ghost Cat Clan</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>4-5:</td>
<td>Gray Wolf Clan</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>6:</td>
<td>Magpie Clan</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>7-8:</td>
<td>Raven Clan</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>9:</td>
<td>Red Fox Clan</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>10:</td>
<td>Seashan Clan</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>11-12:</td>
<td>Snow Fox Tribe</td>
</tr>
</table>
<p>
Note:  QO does not accept personae for the Blood Bear, Wolf-Head and Wolverine Clans.
</p><p>
</p><h3>Position</h3>
<p>
Determine your Position within the clan by rolling one six-sided die.
</p><p>
</p><p>
</p><table border="0">
<tr>
<td>1:</td>
<td>Clan Healer</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>2-3:</td>
<td>Clan Member*</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>3-4:</td>
<td>Warrior</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>6:</td>
<td>Shaman</td>
</tr>
</table>
<p>
*If you roll a result of Clan Member, please specify your role:  artisan, blacksmith, hunter, food gatherer, weaver, potter, fisher, trapper, or some other suitable job.
</p><p>
</p><center><img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/bargrass.gif"/></center>
<p>
</p><h2>II.  Develop a Character</h2>
<p>
Develop a character (your persona--Who would you be if you could daydream yourself into one of the tribes of Norther Barbarians?  Be the "real" you or as different as you please.) and write about him/her.  Prepare a minimum of 3 pages.
</p><p>
This is a biography.  Describe your persona's physical and emotional characteristics, likes and dislikes, whatever makes you uniquely you.  You may use first or third person.
</p><p>
Remember to roll for Clan and Position first; it is the prerequisite for
developing your persona.  You can't write your story without knowing who you are.
</p><p>
(10 points)
</p><p>
</p><center><img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/bargrass.gif"/></center>
<p>
</p><h2>III.  Develop One or More Friend or Relative Characters</h2>
<p>
You needn't be quite so detailed in your descriptions of these other characters.  But remember, you are writing about people you know well.  Describe what your family and/or friends look like, their own character traits, etc.  Again, a minimum of 3 pages.  (5 points)
</p><p>
</p><center><img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/bargrass.gif"/></center>
<p>
</p><h2>IV. Extra Point Requirements (5 points each)</h2>
<p>
Choose and complete the requirements for two (minimum) of the following:
</p><p>
</p><ol>
<li>Write a story of more than 3 pages.
</li><li>Design a costume--Woodland tribe design.
</li><li>Create a costume.
</li><li>Draw a picture of your character.
</li><li>Write an essay about hunting and gathering techniques.  Prepare a minimum of 3 pages.
</li><li>Write a poem about life in your clan.
</li><li>Write an essay on archery.  Prepare a minimum of 3 pages.
</li><li>Create a piece of pottery.
</li><li>Weave a basket.
</li><li>Learn to fish.
</li><li>Draw a map.
</li><li>Learn CPR and basic first-aid.
</li><li>Become a regular blood donor.
</li><li>Become a volunteer.
</li><li>Commit to recycling.
</li><li>Design and/or create an original weaving.
</li><li>Send a story, poem, or art to one of our <a href="/fan-fiction/">zines</a>.
</li></ol>

</p><h2>Advanced Rank</h2>
<p>
There are several advanced ranks possible for Northern Barbarian personae.
</p><p>
<a id="elder" name="elder"></a>
</p><h3>Clan Elder</h3>
<p>
If you have an approved Northern Barbarian persona, you may try for the advanced rank of Elder.  To become a Clan Elder:
</p><p>
</p><ul>
<li>Write a 20-page paper on the Wooland tribe of your choice (For some suggestions, see the map at <a href="http://www.kstrom.net/isk/maps/images/algonmap.gif">http://www.kstrom.net/isk/maps/images/algonmap.gif</a>.).
</li></ul>
<p>
<a id="warleader" name="warleader"></a>
</p><h3>Warleader</h3>
<p>
If you have an approved Clan Elder, you may try for Warleader.  To become a Warleader:
</p><p>
</p><ul>
<li>Write a 20-page paper on warfare as practiced by the Woodland tribe of your choice.
</li></ul>
<p>
<a id="chief" name="chief"></a>
</p><h3>Clan Chief</h3>
<p>
If you have an approved Warleader, you may try for Warleader.  To become a Clan Chief:
</p><p>
</p><ul>
<li>Write a 20-page paper on the Constitution of the Iroquois Federation.
</li></ul>
