</p><h2>Basic Requirements</h2>
<p>
</p><h2>I.  Gifts</h2>
<p>
All Healers have Healing and Empathy.  Determine the nature of each Gift by rolling a six-sided die.  Healer's honor applies; what you roll is what you get.
</p><p>
</p><table border="0">
<tr>
<td>1 = low strength Gift</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>2 - 5 = normal strength Gift</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>6 = very strong Gift</td>
</tr>
</table>
<p>
</p><center><img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/barholly.gif"/></center>
<p>
</p><h2>II.  Develop a Character</h2>
<p>
Develop a character (your persona--Who would you be if you could daydream yourself into Valdemar or somewhere else on Velgarth?  Be the "real" you or as different as you please.) and write about him/her.  Prepare a minimum of 3 pages.
</p><p>
This is a biography.  Describe your persona's physical and emotional characteristics, likes and dislikes, whatever makes you uniquely you.  You may use first or third person.
</p><p>
Remember, to write about your persona <font color="red">before</font> his/her Gifts manifest.  A Healer must learn to cope with his/her Gifts, not have his/her life built around one skill.  Most Gifts manifest themselves late in life; they are not evident at birth.  (10 points)
</p><p>
</p><center><img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/barholly.gif"/></center>
<p>
</p><h2>III.  Develop One or More Friend or Relative Characters</h2>
<p>
You needn't be quite so detailed in your descriptions of these other characters.  But remember, you are writing about people you know well.  Describe what your family and/or friends look like, their own character traits, etc.  Again, a minimum of 3 pages.  (5 points)
</p><p>
</p><center><img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/barholly.gif"/></center>
<p>
</p><h2>IV. Extra Point Requirements (5 points each)</h2>
<p>
Choose and complete the requirements for two (minimum) of the following:
</p><p>
</p><ol>
<li>Learn and certify in CPR.
</li><li>Learn First Aid.
</li><li>Volunteer for 10 hours of service in a health-related field (Blood drive, candy striper, . . . ).
</li><li>Create a list of medical products and their uses (herbs, poultices, etc.).
</li><li>Write a procedure for a specific treatment (trauma, burns., etc.).
</li><li>Draw a map.
</li><li>Become a regular blood donor (Make a commitment to give at least 3x a year).
</li><li>Draw a picture of your or your "other" character(s).
</li><li>Write a story of more than three pages.
</li><li>Illustrate your story.
</li><li>Design a costume.
</li><li>Create a costume.
</li><li>Create an authentic recipe.
</li><li>Start a local <a href="/chapters/">chapter of Queen's Own</a>.
</li><li>Edit a <a href="/fan-fiction/">fanzine/newsletter</a>.
</li><li>Send a story, poem, or art to one of our <a href="/fan-fiction/">zines</a>.
</li></ol>
<p>
</p><center><img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/barholly.gif"/></center>
<p>
</p><center><h2>After you have acquired 25 points, you may earn your Greens!</h2></center>
<p>
<font color="red">Exception</font>:  If you work in the health care
field you may automatically claim full Healer status.  However, please
develop your Velgarth persona so others may interact with you in character.
</p><p>
</p><center><img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/barholly.gif"/></center>

</p><h2>Formal Training</h2>
<p>
Your Healer is at Apprentice level until you enter Formal Training.  To attain higher ranks, follow the instructions below.
</p><p>
<a id="journeyman" name="journeyman"></a>
</p><h3><b>Journeyman Healer</b></h3>
<p>
To become a Journeyman Healer, you must have an approved Healer persona, then
</p><p>
</p><ul>
<li>Write a 20-page paper on the healthcare system of the United States.
</li></ul>
<p>
<a id="master" name="master"></a>
</p><h3><b>Master Healer</b></h3>
<p>
To become a Master Healer, you must have an approved Journeyman Healer persona, then
</p><p>
</p><ul>
<li>Write a 20-page paper on a new medical procedure or drug that is being developed.
</li></ul>
