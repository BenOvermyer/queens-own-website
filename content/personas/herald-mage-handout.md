<font size="4">
</font><center><h1>Herald-Mage Handout</h1></center>
<center><h3>Criteria established by Mercedes Lackey</h3></center>
<center><h3>Based on the <b>Heralds of Valdemar</b> series</h3></center>
<p>
</p><h2>To become a Herald-Mage:</h2>
<p>
</p><ol>
<li>Earn the points necessary to become a <a href="/herald-handout/">Herald</a>.
</li><li>Write a story (minimum of 10 pages) about how your persona discovered that s/he had Mage Gift, and send it to one of the QO zines.
</li></ol>
<p>
You are now at the Apprentice stage.
</p><p>
</p><center><img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/barblln.gif"/></center>
<p>
</p><h2>Herald-Mage, Hedge-Wizard</h2>
<p>
To earn the rank of Herald-Mage, Hedge-Wizard, learn the following stage-magic
tricks well enough to fool your friends:
</p><p>
</p><ul>
<li>Linking Rings
</li><li>Restore Cut Rope
</li><li>Appearing Coins, and
</li><li>a simple Escape Trick.
</li></ul>
<p>
</p><center><img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/barblln.gif"/></center>
<p>
</p><h2>Herald-Mage, Journeyman</h2>
<p>
To earn the rank of Herald-Mage, Journeyman, first qualify for Hedge-Wizard.  Then,
</p><p>
</p><ol>
<li>Write a 20-page paper on the following subject and
</li><li>Submit it to an essay contest or to your History teacher for extra credit.
</li></ol>
<p>
Topic:  Analyze the Constitution, without the Amendments, and explain
how the Amendments have made the original document work in the modern
world.
</p><p>
<b>And</b>
</p><p>
</p><ol>
<li>Learn the following stage-magic tricks:
     <ul>
<li>Dancing Handkerchief
     </li><li>Multiplying Coins
     </li><li>Restoring Cut Newspaper, and
     </li><li>Escape from Handcuffs.
     </li></ul>
</li></ol>
<p>
</p><center><img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/barblln.gif"/></center>
<p>
</p><h2>Herald-Mage, Master</h2>
<p>
To earn the rank of Herald-Mage, Master, first qualify for Hedge-Wizard and Journeyman.  Then
</p><p>
</p><ol>
<li>Write a 20-page paper on one of the following subjects:
     <ul>
<li>Machiavelli's <i>The Prince</i>
</li><li>the Bill of Rights
     </li><li>Elizabeth I as a ruler
     </li><li>the Crusades from the Christian viewpoint or
     </li><li>the Crusades from the Moslem viewpoint.
     </li></ul>
</li></ol>
<p>
Submit it to an essay contest or your History teacher for extra credit.
</p><p>
<b>And</b>
</p><p>
</p><ol>
<li>Write a 20-page paper on "magic" as a form of particle energy and how it is similar to nuclear fusion and/or fission.
</li></ol>
<p>
<b>And</b>
</p><p>
</p><ol>
<li>Learn the following stage-magic tricks:
     <ul>
<li>Hat and Egg
     </li><li>Escape from Ropes
     </li><li>Appearing/Multiplying Ping Pong Balls, and
     </li><li>your choice of Card Tricks.
     </li></ul>
</li></ol>
<p>
</p><center><img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/barblln.gif"/></center>
<p>
</p><h2>Herald-Mage, Adept</h2>
<p>
To earn the rank of Herald-Mage, Adept, first qualify for Hedge-Wizard, Journeyman, and Master.  Then
</p><p>
</p><ol>
<li>Write a 20-page paper on one of the following subjects:
     <ul>
<li>King Arthur as a historical figure
     </li><li>the importance and obligations of the liege-lord in feudal society
     </li><li>Plato's <i>Republic</i>
</li><li>the moral and ethical questions of creation and possession of nuclear weapons
     </li></ul>
</li></ol>
<p>
Submit your paper to an essay contest or your History Teacher for extra credit.
</p><p>
<b>And</b>
</p><p>
</p><ol>
<li>Write a 20-page paper on the moral and ethical questions of possessions and use of Adept-Level power.
</li></ol>
<p>
<b>And</b>
</p><p>
</p><ol>
<li>Write a 20-page paper on the constraints that must be placed on the arbitrator.
</li></ol>
<p>
<b>And</b>
</p><p>
</p><ol>
<li>Learn the following stage-magic tricks:
     <ul>
<li>Cutting a Person in Two Or More Pieces
     </li><li>Appearing Dove
     </li><li>your choice of Card Tricks you don't already know, and
     </li><li>your choice of Close-Up Magic Tricks you don't already know.
     </li></ul>
</li></ol>
<p>
Lest you think I am being too hard on those who would be Herald-Mages, I would like to point out
that both Ellen Guon and myself qualify for Adept.  Like aspiring
to <a href="/kalen-edral-handout/">Kal'enedral</a>, if you want something extraordinary, you will have to make an
extraordinary effort.
</p><p>
</p><center><img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/barblln.gif"/></center>
<p>
</p><h2>Special Notes for Herald-Mages</h2>
<p>
</p><ul>
<li>As proof of your qualifications, send a brief description of each magic trick and tell how you learned it (i.e., from a book, a magic shop, a friend . . . )
<p>
</p></li><li>Send a copy of each 20-page paper and tell where and when it was published.  (Finding a publisher is part of the challenge.  If the piece is too long for any local publisher to accept, let me know.  Queen's Own has an online nonfiction journal, <a href="/compass-rose/about/">The Compass Rose</a> to take such materials.  Published books count in place of papers if the book is on the same topic as the paper.)<p>

</p></li><li>For Helpful Hints, click <a href="/persona-handouts/#help">here</a>.
</li></ul>
