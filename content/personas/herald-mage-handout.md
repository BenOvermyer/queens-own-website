## Herald-Mage Handout

### Criteria established by Mercedes Lackey

### Based on the **Heralds of Valdemar** series

## To become a Herald-Mage:

1. Earn the points necessary to become a [Herald](/herald-handout/).
2. Write a story (minimum of 10 pages) about how your persona discovered that s/he had Mage Gift, and send it to one of the QO zines.

You are now at the Apprentice stage.

<img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/barblln.gif"/>

## Herald-Mage, Hedge-Wizard

To earn the rank of Herald-Mage, Hedge-Wizard, learn the following stage-magic tricks well enough to fool your friends:

- Linking Rings
- Restore Cut Rope
- Appearing Coins, and
- a simple Escape Trick.

<img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/barblln.gif"/>

## Herald-Mage, Journeyman

To earn the rank of Herald-Mage, Journeyman, first qualify for Hedge-Wizard. Then,

1. Write a 20-page paper on the following subject and
2. Submit it to an essay contest or to your History teacher for extra credit.

Topic: Analyze the Constitution, without the Amendments, and explain how the Amendments have made the original document work in the modern world.

**And**

1. Learn the following stage-magic tricks:
  - Dancing Handkerchief
  - Multiplying Coins
  - Restoring Cut Newspaper, and
  - Escape from Handcuffs.

<img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/barblln.gif"/>

## Herald-Mage, Master

To earn the rank of Herald-Mage, Master, first qualify for Hedge-Wizard and Journeyman. Then

1. Write a 20-page paper on one of the following subjects:
  - Machiavelli's *The Prince*
  - the Bill of Rights
  - Elizabeth I as a ruler
  - the Crusades from the Christian viewpoint or
  - the Crusades from the Moslem viewpoint.

Submit it to an essay contest or your History teacher for extra credit.

**And**

1. Write a 20-page paper on "magic" as a form of particle energy and how it is similar to nuclear fusion and/or fission.

**And**

1. Learn the following stage-magic tricks:
  - Hat and Egg
  - Escape from Ropes
  - Appearing/Multiplying Ping Pong Balls, and
  - your choice of Card Tricks.

<img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/barblln.gif"/>

## Herald-Mage, Adept

To earn the rank of Herald-Mage, Adept, first qualify for Hedge-Wizard, Journeyman, and Master. Then

1. Write a 20-page paper on one of the following subjects:
  - King Arthur as a historical figure
  - the importance and obligations of the liege-lord in feudal society
  - Plato's *Republic*
  - the moral and ethical questions of creation and possession of nuclear weapons

Submit your paper to an essay contest or your History Teacher for extra credit.

**And**

1. Write a 20-page paper on the moral and ethical questions of possessions and use of Adept-Level power.

**And**

1. Write a 20-page paper on the constraints that must be placed on the arbitrator.

**And**

1. Learn the following stage-magic tricks:
  - Cutting a Person in Two Or More Pieces
  - Appearing Dove
  - your choice of Card Tricks you don't already know, and
  - your choice of Close-Up Magic Tricks you don't already know.

Lest you think I am being too hard on those who would be Herald-Mages, I would like to point out that both Ellen Guon and myself qualify for Adept. Like aspiring to [Kal'enedral](/kalen-edral-handout/), if you want something extraordinary, you will have to make an extraordinary effort.

## Special Notes for Herald-Mages

- As proof of your qualifications, send a brief description of each magic trick and tell how you learned it (i.e., from a book, a magic shop, a friend . . . )
- Send a copy of each 20-page paper and tell where and when it was published. (Finding a publisher is part of the challenge. If the piece is too long for any local publisher to accept, let me know. Queen's Own has an online nonfiction journal, [The Compass Rose](/compass-rose/about/) to take such materials. Published books count in place of papers if the book is on the same topic as the paper.)
