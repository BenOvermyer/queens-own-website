</p><h2>Basic Requirements</h2>
<p>
</p><h2>I.  Determine Your Guild</h2>
<p>
Roll two (2) six-sided dice and consult the following chart to determine your Guild.  These are the Guilds that are open for persona creation at this time:
</p><p>
</p><ol>
<li>Cloth Merchants Guild
</li><li>Dyers Guild
</li><li>Leather and Furrier's Guild
</li><li>Leatherworkers Guild
</li><li>Mercenary Guild
</li><li>Needleworkers Guild
</li><li>Potters Guild
</li><li>Traders Guild
</li><li>Weavers Guild
</li><li>Woodworkers Guild
</li><li>Wrightguild
</li></ol>
<p>
</p><h2>II.  Develop a Character</h2>
<p>
Develop a character (your persona--Who would you be if you could daydream yourself into one of the Velgarth Guilds? Be the "real" you or as different as you please.) and write about him/her. Prepare a minimum of 3 pages.
</p><p>
This is a biography. Describe your persona's physical and emotional characteristics, likes and dislikes, whatever makes you uniquely you. You may use first or third person.
</p><p>
Remember to identify your Guild <font color="red">first</font>, then write your stories.  Most people go into a Guild that one of their parents or another close relative is in.  (10 points.)
</p><p>
</p><center><img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/barcastl.gif"/></center>
<p>
</p><h2>III.  Develop One or More Friend or Relative Characters</h2>
<p>
You needn't be quite so detailed in your descriptions of these other characters. But remember, you are writing about people you know well. Describe what your family and/or friends look like, their own character traits, etc. Again, a minimum of 3 pages. (5 points)
</p><p>
</p><center><img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/barcastl.gif"/></center>
<p>
</p><h2>IV.  Extra Point Requirements (5 points each)</h2>
<p>
Choose and complete the requirements for two (minimum) of the following:
</p><p>
</p><ol>
<li>Write a story of more than 3 pages.
</li><li>Learn how to drive a cart pulled by a horse or other animal.
</li><li>Create a costume.
</li><li>Make a list of different colors of dyes and how they are created in a medieval society.
</li><li>Write an essay about the use of leather and fur in clothing.  Prepare a minimum of 3 pages.
</li><li>Make a belt, vest, book cover or other item out of leather.
</li><li>Start a local <a href="/chapters/">chapter of Queen's Own</a>.
</li><li>Start a <a href="/fan-fiction/">fanzine</a> for Mercedes Lackey fanfiction.
</li><li>Join the SCA or similar Living History Re-enactment Group that has ranks for fighters.
</li><li>Embroider something or create something else that involves needlework.
</li><li>Create a pot either entirely by hand or using a potter's wheel.
</li><li>Write a report about the economics of a medieval society. Prepare a minimum of 3 pages.
</li><li>Send a story, poem, or art to one of our <a href="/fan-fiction/">zines</a>.
</li><li>Draw a map of your Guild's trade routes or districts.
</li><li>Write an essay discussing the various skills that traders need.  Prepeare a minimum of 3 pages.
</li><li>Weave cloth on a loom.
</li><li>Carve something out of wood.
</li><li>Build a piece of furniture.
</li></ol>

</p><h2>Advanced Rank</h2>
<p>
Guild members may advance in rank to Journeyman and then to Master.
</p><p>
<a id="journeyman" name="journeyman"></a>
</p><h3>Journeyman</h3>
<p>
To become a Journeyman Guilder, first create an approved Guild persona, then:
</p><p>
</p><ul>
<li>Write a 20-page paper on something in your Guild's specialty.
</li></ul>
<p>
<a id="master" name="master"></a>
</p><h3>Master</h3>
<p>
To become a Master Guilder, first complete the requirements for Journeyman Guilder, then:
</p><p>
</p><ul>
<li>Create something appropriate to your Guild and
</li><li>Write a 20-page paper explaining what you made and how you made it.
</li></ul>
