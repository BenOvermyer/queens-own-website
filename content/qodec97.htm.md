Dear Misty Fans,
</p><p>
Coming this month from Baen is <i>Four &amp; Twenty Blackbirds</i>, Book IV of <b>Bardic Voices</b>. The cover illustration is bv Darrell K. Sweet.
</p><p>
"A magical murderer is loose in Alanda. The victims are always women, always lower-class, and the weapon is always a three-sided stiletto, most often found among Church regalia. But the killers are never churchmen, and they always commit suicide immediately after the bloody deed.
</p><p>
"Tat Rufen is just a simple constable. But he really cares about his job, and when one of these murder/suicides happens on his beat he becomes obsessed. His superiors don't care -- the victims will never be missed, and their murderers are already justly dead. But every instinct Tat Rufen has cries out that he has seen only one small piece of a bigger and much nastier puzzle . . . "
</p><p>
Other books in the <b>Bardic Voices</b> series are <i>The Lark &amp; the Wren</i>, <i>The Robin &amp; the Kestrel</i>, and <i>The Eagle &amp; the Nightingales</i>. For more about Ardis and the Kingsford fire, read <b>Bardic Choices</b>: <i>A Cast of Corbies</i>, co-written with Josepha Sherman.
</p><p>
<i>Four &amp; Twenty Blackbirds</i> is a hardcover, with a list price of $22.
</p><p>
Good news for all of us who enjoyed <i>Owlflight</i>, there will be an <i>Owlflight II</i>. And, Michael Longcor is working on an <i>Owlflight</i> album. 
</p><p>
Holiday cards for Misty and Larry can be sent to them in care of their business address: Highflight [Ed. note 2005: Out-of-date info withheld.]. If you would like to show your appreciation for their books with more than a card, consider a donation to <b>The Alex Foundation</b>, their favorite charity. Misty says, "<b>The Alex Foundation</b> supports the research of Dr. Irene Pepperberg of the University of Arizona in her research into the intelligence, cognitive, and speaking abilities of African Grey Parrots. What she has learned so far has contributed to great advances in the treatment and therapy of learning-disabled children. Dr. Pepperberg raises more than half the money for her research herself, criss-crossing the country to lecture and give demonstrations--on top of her full-time research position!	.
</p><p>
" . . . This is a unique opportunity for ordinary folk to support basic research into patterns of learning. All contributions to The Alex Foundation are tax deductible . . . "
</p><p>
Send your donations to The Alex Foundation [Ed. note 2005: Out-of-date info withheld.].
</p><p>
Dr. Pepperberg's web page is at [Ed. note 2005: Out-of-date info withheld.].  Check Firebird's site ([Ed. note 2005: Out-of-date info withheld.]) for information on a "silent auction" fund raiser. The auction features mostly items hand made by Misty or Larry. (Mostly by Misty, she says, since her hobbies are handicrafts of various sorts.)
</p><p>
A parrot named Grey plays an important part in Misty's story "Grey" in issue #37 of <i>Marion Zimmcr Bradley's FANTASY Magazine</i>. A single issue is [Ed. note 2005: Out-of-date info withheld.]. Send your order to [Ed. note 2005: Out-of-date info withheld.], checks payable to Marion Zimmer Bradley.
</p><p>
Misty provides the lyrics for the songs on "Magic, Moondust, &amp; Melancholy," a cassette available from Firebird. One of the songs, "Gil-shalos," particularly intrigued me so I read the Barbara Hambly books that inspired it. If you too are a fan of those who battle the Dark, you'll be as pleased as I was to discover that a new Darwath book by Barbara, <i>Icefalcon's Quest</i>, is coming in February. <i>Icefalcon's Quest</i> is a Del Ray hardcover.
</p><p>
Barbara Hambly will be the Guest of Honor at Fantasy Worlds Festival in Berkeley next Labor Day weekend. Details will be available on the MZB web page [Ed. note 2005: Out-of-date info withheld.] and in future <i>MZB FANTASY Magazines</i> and QO newsletters.
</p><p>
Do your holiday shopping at Firebird Arts &amp; Music. Their latest catalog has just been mailed. If you're not yet on their mailing list, send [Ed. note 2005: Out-of-date info withheld.], or browse through their on-line catalog at [Ed. note 2005: Out-of-date info withheld.]. Truly, there's something for everyone. Call [Ed. note 2005: Out-of-date info withheld.] to place an order.

</p><h2>COLLEGIUM NEWS:</h2>
<p>
Are you interested in creating a persona who will be recognized in the QO newsletter?  All it takes is your imagination
and your time. Develop your persona by daydreaming yourself into the world of Velgarth. You can be the real you or as different as you please. Write at least three pages about yourself; what are your emotional characteristics?  What do you look like?  What makes you happy?  What are you afraid of?
</p><p>
Now, create your family and friends.  Describe their likes and dislikes, your relationship to them, their strongest character traits.  Again, write at least three pages.  (A page should be double-spaced, on one side of 8.5 by 11 inch paper or a similar size.  If you have to handwrite or print your material, please be as neat as possible.)
</p><p>
Extra point requirements vary depending on what role you've chosen for yourself. Each category has requirements that will add to the growth of your character. All of us can be a~volunteer and learn CPR, and many of us can be a regular blood donor.
</p><p>
Send a SASE to the QO address for specific handouts--Herald, Healer, Bard, Mercenary, Shin'a'in. Tayledras, White Winds Sorcerer/ess--with more information and a <a href="https://queensown.org/mlrel2.htm">release form</a> so you can write a story set in Misty's world. (Biographical information, even in the form of a story, does not need a release form.)
</p><p>
Come, enroll in the Collegium!
</p><p>
By the way, you may NOT create a Kestra'chern persona.  Re-read the Gryphon books. What skills do you expect to learn for extra credit?
</p><p>

</p><h3>From Herald Housemother:</h3>
<p>
May the coming holidays be a time of joy for all of you and your family and friends.  May we all get new books by Misty and the time to read them leisurely.
</p><p>
Most of my family gathered at our house for Thanksgiving dinner. We fed 30 people, ranging in age from 87 to 16 months. The next day our son Frank decorated the yard and outside of our house with white lights on the eaves, shrubs, garage and workshop. Steve and I will add wreaths and red bows. Now it's time to plan my cookie baking.
</p><p>
If you sent a SASE for a specific handout and never got it, please send a postcard telling me what you want. The copier refused to work at one time and requests were put to one side until it was fixed. It's now fixed, but I've misplaced your SASEs.
</p><p>
I have a favor to ask--I'm scheduled for laser treatment (because of glaucoma) on January 12th. Please send your prayers, healing energy, white light, best wishes, whatever you believe may help, because I'm frankly terrified. Intellectually I know it's painless, I know it's routine, my mom had it done when she was 84 years old. Emotionally, the idea makes me feel ill. Help!
