Dear Misty Fans,
</p><p>
Isn't <i>Magic's Price</i> wonderful?  I hesitate to say too much about it, in case any of you are still waiting for the opportunity to read it, but I found it well wroth the wait, the many tissues I sniffled into while reading it, and even the headache I had the next day from staying up most of the night to finish it.  And the dedication pleases me immensely, I see no reason to pretend otherwise!
</p><p>
<i>By the Sword</i> is finished, all 600+ pages, and at the printer.  This is the "bridge" book, which brings together old friends from the <i>Arrows</i> books and Kethry's granddaughter Kerowyn.  It is the first of abother trilogy, I'm glad to say.  
</p><p>
Work is progressing nicely on <i>The Elvenbane</i>, Misty's collaboration with Andre Norton.  The elves in this story are very different from Kory and his friends in <i>Knight of Ghosts and Shadows</i>.
</p><p>
<b>Good news from Firebird:</b> There will be a Vanyel Tape!  The songs will be performed by Larry Warner, singing as Vanyel's nephew, the Bard Medren.
</p><p>
<u>And</u> the songs on "Heralds, Harpers and Havoc" will be out in songbook form for only eight dollars.  Firebird will send you the words and chords as soon as they receive your order.  THe complete songbook will follow as soon as it is finished.
</p><p>
The <i>Oathbound</i> tape will be available after a few more songs; please be patient.
</p><p>
You may call 1-800-752-0494 to order a catalog or place an order. (They've just added six new Bayfilk tapes.) Or write to <b>Firebird</b> at <b>P.O. Box 14785, Portland, OR  97214-9998</b>.
</p><p>
<b>BOOK RELEASE DATE:</b> Coming in August: <i>Children of the Night</i>.
</p><p>
The quote from TOR's ad for <i>Children of the Night</i> in the July issue of <i>LOCUS</i> is: "Diana Tregarde is new to New York, and to her Guardianship--but she's ready to defend her friends, and her adopted city, from any Evil--even vampires!"  And in the same ad, a quote from Marion Zimmer Bradley: "It's a delight . . . to know that a writer whose work I've loved all along has written something so fresh and original."
</p><p>
<i>Knight of Ghosts and Shadows</i> got a lovely review from Scott Winnett in the same issue, ending with: " An enchanting romp, with wonderful characters and a deep appreciation for the magic underneath our everyday lives."
</p><p>
The titles of the chapters of <i>Knight</i>, by the way, are names of folk songs or Celtic fiddle tunes.  Misty says she and Ellen have ideas for at least two more Eric Banyon books.  Write to <b>Baen Publihshing, 260 Fifth Avenue, New York, NY 10001</b> if you enjoyed <i>Knight</i>; tell them you want more!
</p><p>
Chris Johnson's "The Helpful Handout of How to Become a Herald" has proven to be very helpful--and very popular.  It's still available [Ed. note 2002: Out-of-date information deleted.  Visit <a href="/qohandouts.htm">http://www.dragonlordsnet.com/qohandouts.htm</a> for current information.].  Thanks, Chris, from all of us.  We appreciate your hard work.
</p><p>
[Ed. note 2002: out-of-date membership information delated.  See below for current instructions.]  I have no objection to your sharing a newsletter with friends.  I do ask that you join Queen's Own if you intend to work for Herald status or use any of our services, like fan messages or mentors.
</p><p>
Queen's Own membership buttons are still available. [Ed. note 2002:  Out-of-date information deleted.]  
</p><p>
Patricia Riggs and I thank all of you for your responses to our cries for help.  We've had offers from game-players, con-goers, filkers, and zine collectors, all of whom are eager to share their enthusiasm with others.  Their names and addresses will be in the September newsletter.  The wide, weird, and wonderful world of fannish activities is even more fun when shared.
</p><p>
Ellen Wells, [Ed. note 2002: Out-of-date contact information deleted.  Send current reciptes to <a href="mailto:Legend@malcor.com">Legend@malcor.com</a> or to the QO P.O. box.]is still collecting recipes for a Valdemar Cookbook.  Include a SASE if you want your recipe(s) returned.  Let's see how many variations of Trail Bread we can create.  And don't forget desserts.  What do you suppose the HErald version of S'Mores is like?  (Thanks from Ellen to Jessica Woods for her offer of help.)

</p><h2>HERALD REGISTRY:</h2>
<p>
</p><ol>
<li>A special welcome to our first Herald from a distant land.  Jeannie R. Finlay, from far away Australia, introduces us to <b>Herald Siona Kenarch</b> and her Companion Thowra.  Herald Siona's gifts are Mindspeech, Healing, Fetching, and some Farsight.  Jeannie sent a description of her persona, a story, a poem, two articles, a letter home, and plans of Siona's town and her room at the Collegium.<p>
</p></li><li>Welcome, <b>Herald Shanayah Dawn</b>!  We are pleased to have you and your Companion Melantha join us.  Herald Shay's gifts are Fetching, Mindspeech, and Empathy.  Tammy Haffen fulfilled her requirements by creating and making a costume, writing about Shanayah and her family, and drawing a diagram of Shanayah's home.<p>
</p></li><li><b>Herald Mylitta</b> comes to us with the talents of Firestarter and some Mindspeech.  She was chosen by the stallion Sieken.  Dana Evans sent character sketches of Mylitta and three of her friends, drawings, a blueprint, and a story.<p>
</p></li><li>Donna Proffitt sent character sketches of <b>Herald Ariadhne</b>, chosen by the mare Eodain, and gifted with Mind HEaling and Close Range Telepathy, and her two closest Herald friends.  She included a story, two poems, a recipe, and a floorplan of Ariadhne's home.<p>
</p></li><li>Welcome, <b>Herald Katerynne Lionsmane</b>, chosen by Alyshondra and gifted with Mindspeech and Animal Mindspeech.  Melinda Layden sent a detailed description of her persona, a wonderful Companion done in origami, a poem, and a tape of two original songs.<p>
</p></li><li><b>Herald Darii</b> was chosen by the stallion Neptine.  Her gift is Mindspeech.  She is most unusual for a Herald, as she is one of a set of triplets, all of whom were Chosen!  Nell Beatty sent persona sketches of the triplets and their family, a story of how they were Chosen, a map, a poem and a computer generated picture.<p>
</p></li><li>Welcome, <b>Herald Alanna Meris</b>.  Andrea Kylara tells us that Herald Alanna was Chosen by the mare Cara.  Her gifts are Empathy and Animal Mindspeech.  Andrea sent character sketches of Alanna and her little sister Kanna, photos of herself in her Herald whties, a map of Valdemar showing Alanna's hometown, and drawings of a costume and Alanna.<p>
</p></li><li><b>Herald Dezalina</b> was Chosen by the stallion Kandar, and has the gift of Mindspeech.  Helena Rotell sent information on the care and feeding of horses, a picture of herself on her horse Cocoa, a poem, character sketches, and a story.<p>
</p></li><li>A belated but very sincere welcome to <b>Herald Cameron Tarn</b>, Chosen many months ago by the Companion Starmer.  Tammy Denault tells us that Tarn's gifts are Fetching and Mindspeech.  Tammy sent a character outline, a story, a poem, maps of Tarn's home, and pictures of herself in her Herald's whites.<p>
(I discovered Tammy's Herald material, postmarked April 17th, just a few days ago!  To Tammy, my embarrassed apologies.  To the rest of QO, my promise that I will try very hard to keep such a thing from happening again.)
</p></li></ol>

THank you all for sharing so much of yourselves with Misty and me.

Hi from Herald Housemother:
</p><p>
Among my fancy titles is the word "dogsbody." A dogsbody is a menial, a drudge.  I have the fun of being editor and president, I also get to do most of the work.  Any mail that is addressed to Queen's Own is opened by me and read and answered by me.  This is still a kitchen table top operation, and a cat frequently climbs into my lap to chase my pen as I answer your letters.
</p><p>
On the subject of mistakes in the newsletter:
</p><p>
"I think that I shall never see<br/>
An issue totally error-free<br/>
I read. I proof, I read some more,<br/>
But yet, I see when it's left my door,<br/>
That a zip code is wrong, a name's misspelled,<br/>
A dare is reversed, and typos as well!<br/>
It's not that I'm not trying my best,<br/>
It's just that mistakes are like pet hairs--they don't show until you're out in public and can be embarrassed by their presence."<br/>
'Nuff said!
