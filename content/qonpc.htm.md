<p>
<a href="https://dragonlords.fans/bkmlvs.htm"><img align="left" alt="" border="0" loading="lazy" src="/ml4.gif"/></a>These 
are the approved additional personae (NPCs) for Queen's Own.  To create a recognized NPC, you have 
to do everything on a <a href="/qohandouts.htm">handout</a>, just as you would if you were creating an initial persona. 
(You do not have to do this if you simply want to make up characters to use 
in stories.  The NPCs listed on this page represent only those characters for whom 
the member has done the additional work.)  If you have an 
approved NPC persona who is not listed here, please contact 
<a href="mailto:Legend@malcor.com">me</a>.  
</p><p>
If a name is not linked and does not have an asterisk (*), then we do not know how to reach that person.  If you know him/her, please have him/her contact us with a current address!
</p><p>
*=e-mail withheld by request or unavailable.
</p><p>
<a href="https://dragonlords.fans/bkmlvs.htm"><img align="right" alt="" border="0" loading="lazy" src="/ml5.gif"/></a>Each 
person may have as many NPC personae as s/he wishes.  The NPC will only be
listed on this page, however, if the club member has done all of the 
required work on the <a href="/qohandouts.htm">handout</a> for that character 
type.
</p><p>
Dates following the names indicate the date of publication of the 
newsletter in which the persona was recognized.  Herald-Mages have 
multiple dates, indicating their date of acceptance as a Herald and 
the date they earned each of their Herald-Mage ranks.  
</p><p>
