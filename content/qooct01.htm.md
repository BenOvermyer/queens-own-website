Heyla, Misty fans!
</p><p>
We�ve heard from a number of you over the last few weeks.  Our hearts go out to all who have been touched by the tragedy of September 11th.  We are inspired by the stories you have told us about how you have found various ways to help.  It is comforting to know that the Heraldic spirit is alive and well among the members of Queen�s Own.  
</p><p>
We want to take this opportunity to acknowledge all of the modern-day Heralds that we�ve seen in action during the past few weeks:  the firefighters and police officers who lost their lives trying to save others, the people who helped their colleagues to safety and those who stayed behind so friends and strangers would not die alone, the rescue workers and medical personnel who have worked so long and hard, helping the survivors and reaching out to the families and friends of those who did not survive . . . We have also heard stories of heroic animal companions:  the guide dog who led a blind woman down and out of the building from one of the floors in the 70s, the rescue dogs who worked alongside their human counterparts . . . The tales go on and on . . . 
</p><p>
We also ask your continued vigilance in combating the hate crimes that have occurred in some areas.  Just as the Valdemarans in the <i>Storms</i> series needed to learn to overcome their prejudices against the Karsites after centuries of war, we modern folk of the real world need to remind ourselves and others that a few bad people do not make all members of a given ethnic group evil.  We encourage you to reach out to the Middle Eastern people you know and give them a friendly word, support their businesses, and otherwise encourage them in this difficult time.
</p><p>
In fan club news, we are still working to reestablish our chat list.  We think we�ve found a solution to this, and we hope to be contacting you about it soon.
</p><p>
Misty has been extremely busy publishing.  <i>Brightly Burning</i> and <i>The Fire Rose</i> are out in paperback.  <i>Take a Thief</i>, the new Valdemar novel about Skif, is due out this month in hardcover.  The paperback for <i>Beyond World�s End</i>, the continuation of the Bedlam series, and <i>Spirits White as Lightning</i> (with Rosemary Edgehill) are scheduled for release in December.  <i>Shadow of the Lion</i> is expected in March 2002, and <i>Elvenborn</i> is slated to appear in April 2002.  As you add to your collections and do your holiday shopping, please think about ordering through the Mercedes Lackey section of the Dragonlords� Bookstore at <a href="https://dragonlords.fans/bkfanml.htm">http://www.dragonlordsnet.com/bkfanml.htm</a>.  A portion of every purchase goes to help pay for our website, PO box and all the other expenses of running this club, which is how we have been able to keep the memberships free so far.  We truly appreciate your support. 
</p><p>
