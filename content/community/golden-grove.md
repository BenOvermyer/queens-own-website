## The California Chapter of Queen's Own

<img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/GGlogo.png" width="100" height="100"/>

Golden Grove is the California Chapter of Queen's Own. It is currently up for adoption. If you would like to adopt this chapter, contact Danya at [shashtah@gmail.com](mailto:shashtah@gmail.com).

## History

Golden Grove used to be called "The Herald's Hall of California". The chapter was founded in October 1989 by Christa Miller, which makes it one of the oldest chapters of QO. The original newsletter was called "The California Arrow". The "Arrow" came out bimonthly and included con dates, book reviews, biographies of members, places to go, and so forth.

## Links of Interest

Golden Grove is the California chapter of [Queen's Own](/), the official Mercedes Lackey Appreciation newsletter. No infringements of her or anyone else's copyrights is intended. Golden Grove is a not-for-profit fan newsletter formerly published by Aurora Nelson.
