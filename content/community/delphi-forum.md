Members can chat about Misty's books and other things in the Queen's Own Delphi Forum. When you join Queen's Own, you will be issued an invitation to join the private forum. You may visit there at any time. The only strict rule is that there is absolutely no posting of fan fiction. This includes talking in dialog. You can talk about a story you are writing, but you cannot post the story. (Sorry, that's the way Misty's lawyers want it.) We'll eventually have sections to talk about character development, cons, and anything else that interests our members.
