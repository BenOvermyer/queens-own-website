</p><h2>How to Start a Chapter</h2>
<p>
If you would rather start a chapter from scratch, contact <a href="mailto:shashtah@gmail.com">Herald-Mage Adept Danya Winterborn</a> and tell her your idea for the chapter.  Decide what you want to do.  Chapters may:
</p><p>
</p><ul>
<li>Use the Queen's Own name.
</li><li>Publish a hardcopy <a href="/fan-fiction/">fanzine</a> or newsletter that contains fan fiction.
</li><li>Publish a nonfiction e-mail newsletter.
</li><li>Put up a website.
</li><li>Host a chatroom.
</li><li>Form a chatlist.
</li><li>Organize trips to Ren. Faires, Cons, and other places of interest.
</li><li>Hold Queen's Own Festivals.
</li><li>Run a QO section at a Con.
</li><li>Come up with other ideas of fun stuff to do with other Misty fans.
</li></ul>
<p>
A chapter may advertise for members in the QO newsletter and solicit members on the QO chatlist.  A chapter can also run an ad about the newsletter or fanzine (if any) that the group publishes.  Contact <a href="mailto:shashtah@gmail.com">Herald-Mage Adept Danya Winterborn</a> to place ads or if you would like me to interview you about your organization for the QO newsletter.
