## How to Start a Chapter

If you would rather start a chapter from scratch, contact [Herald-Mage Adept Danya Winterborn](mailto:shashtah@gmail.com) and tell her your idea for the chapter. Decide what you want to do. Chapters may:

- Use the Queen's Own name.
- Publish a hardcopy [fanzine](/fan-fiction/) or newsletter that contains fan fiction.
- Publish a nonfiction e-mail newsletter.
- Put up a website.
- Host a chatroom.
- Form a chatlist.
- Organize trips to Ren. Faires, Cons, and other places of interest.
- Hold Queen's Own Festivals.
- Run a QO section at a Con.
- Come up with other ideas of fun stuff to do with other Misty fans.

A chapter may advertise for members in the QO newsletter and solicit members on the QO chatlist. A chapter can also run an ad about the newsletter or fanzine (if any) that the group publishes. Contact [Herald-Mage Adept Danya Winterborn](mailto:shashtah@gmail.com) to place ads or if you would like me to interview you about your organization for the QO newsletter.
