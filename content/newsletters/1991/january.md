Dear Misty Fans,

Greetings from Misty and Larry! They say they intended to send Christmas cards but got sidetracked . . . Funny how a wedding and honeymoon make one forget everything else. If there isn't a more personal greeting from them tucked in with this issue, it will be included in the February newsletter.

*The Mage Winds* trilogy, the books that take us back to Valdemar to the time of the events of *Arrow's Fall*, will have illustrations by Larry Dixon. The first book, *Winds of Fate*, has a release date of November first. Because it will be in hard cover, the price will be $21.95.

*By the Sword*, the book that brings Kethry's granddaughter Kerowyn to Valdemar, will reach your bookstore any time after the fifteenth of this month.

The Firebird Winter flyer is a special Mercedes Lackey edition. Here you will find all of Misty's novels and tapes, Julie Bowman's beautiful white fabric Companions and many-coloured fire lizards, Black Gryphon and Companion T-shirts designed by Misty and Larry, and even Skandranon, the Black Gryphon himself, as a pewter sculpture. Also in pewter are pendants and pins. The large pendant design is the crest of Valdemar. I own nearly everything in the flyer, and I wholeheartedly recommend it all. Terri Leee sent me extra flyers; [Ed. note 2002: Out-of-date information withheld.]. If you prefer to write to Firebird Arts & Music directly, their address is [Ed. note 2002: Out-of-date information withheld; the Firebird website is at [http://www.firebirdarts.com](http://www.firebirdarts.com).]

The crest of Valdemar design is a winged horse with broken chains. Its origin is with King Valdemar's son. He was also a Herald-Mage and was called Windrider because his manifestation was a winged horse. (A manifestation is the form one creates to fight a mage-duel.) Windrider was captured and bound and drained magically. Sunsinger and Shadowdancer freed him but after that his manifestation always appeared with broken chains. When Windrider became king he decreed that this would be the arms of Valdemar, signifying that no one would be able to conquer the kingdom, though they might temporarily defeat her.

If you've forgotten the story of King Valdemar, you can look it up in *Arrows of the Queen*.

Colorado Misty fans can contact **Lisa Rathbun**, [Ed. note 2002: Out-of-date information withheld.], for information on chapter meetings.

If you live in the Ohio area, contact **Celeste Frazier**, [Ed. note 2002: Out-of-date information withheld.].

The purpost of this Queen's Own newsletter is to keep you informed of Misty's and Larry's books, tapes, convention appearances, and other projects. The fan messages allow you to contact each other. Why should you join or start a local chapter of Queen's Own? There's so much more to be done!

For instance, you can have a question and answer column with questions ranging from "If Talamir hadn't been killed, thus freeing Rolan to choose another Herald, what would Talia's life have been like?" to "Who of Misty's characters is your favorite adn why?" and "If *The Last Herald-Mage* were going to be a movie, whom would you cast as Vanyel? Stefen? Savil? Medren? Treesa?"

You can have LOC's (Letters of Comment) about other authors, movies, TV shows worth watching, local cons, gaming news . . . whatever is of interest; you can start a Round Robin and print te finished story; you can be even more creative. How about an interview with Diana Tregarde? A job resume by Eric Banyon? An article on what the well-dressed Herald wears off duty?

Not ready to write for one of our zines? Send a poem or short story (*very* short) to the chapter newsletter.

A newsletter is limited only by the editor's imagination and the amount of available space. I'm not willing to attempt more than our current five and a half pages, not for the dues I charge and not with the time I have. The local chapters fill a definite need. Join one and give it your support!

One note of warning: running even a small club and writing a newsletter will take more time and money than you allow--no matter how much you allow!
