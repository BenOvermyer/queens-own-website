Dear Misty Fans,

*Jinx High*, Misty's third Diana Tregarde book, will be out sometime this summer. *Winds of Fare*, the first of **The Mage Wars** trilogy has a release date of November first. In the meantime, try a copy of *Marion Zimmer Bradley's FANTASY Magazine*. Diana Tregarde and Andre appear in issue 10, and Misty has a slightly different kind of horror tale in issue 9. It's called "Roadkill," and you'll never look at roadside trash the same way again. A subscription to **FANTASY Magazine** is \[Ed. note 2003: Out-of-date subscription information withheld.\]

Misty and Larry are raising an injured kesstrel named Vincent who accompanied them in the RV on their honeymoon. Here's news about Cinvent from Misty, courtesy of Michelle Lutz and Genie:

"Just before we left Las Vegas we found a vet who is also a falconer: He treated Vincent for a cracked beak, and x-rayed the wing, and it's good news! We'll be able to release him in about a year! Meanwhile he is coming along well as a trained falcon; comes to the fist for food now, and goes happily into his cage under his own power. In fact, this afternoon he was waiting, pacing at the door of the cage when he saw me with dinner, and POUNCED on the fist."

A few days later . . . "Oh, anybody know how to get a mouse out of a Suzuki? We bought Vincent three, but the box came open and we only found two . . . gonna be a mouse-cicle for Vincent if we don't find it soon."

Five days later . . . "We found the mouse-cicle, hidden under the seat of Skandranon, the Black Gryphon (our unstoppable Suzuki Sidekick). Vincent said 'Oooh! A frozen treat, for lil' ol' me?!' and ate it. Deferring details of falcon mouse-eating habits for those of you with weaker stomachs . . . Larry pointed out that we certainly do have a unique relationship, what with dialog like:

"Honey, I found that dead mous in Skan . . . "

"You did? Excellent! It isn't bloated yet is it?"

"Nope, looks just fine!"

"Thus the reasons falconers are not invited to dinner parties."

Jody Lee, the artist who did the covers for *The Heralds of Valdemar* and *The Last Herald-Mage*, has the cover roughed out for *Winds of Fate*. It will feature Elspeth and Darkwind k'Sheyna and Gwena on the front cover and some nasty Changechildren and Mournelithe Falconsbane on the back. And interior illustrations by Larry Dixon.

Who is Mournelithe Falconsbane? He's the "sexy villain" of the trilogy. Let Misty describe him: "If you can imagine a corss between Vincent of B&B and David Bowie, with a generous helping of Rutgar Hauer, you have Mournelithe Falconsbane."

Jody says the Vanyel series prints should be ready for sale any day. \[Ed. note 2003: Out-of-date information withheld.\] The prints are of the whole image--front and back covers. \[Ed. note 2003: Out-of-date information withheld.\]

### Hello from Herald Housemother:

It's *still* nagging time: **Please**, *please*, **put a return address and your full name on every letter you send to me or a penpal**. Check your address label for misspelling. If a newsletter is returned to me by the post office, I look through my records to make sure I spelled everything correctly. If the rejected address matches the one on file, my responsibility is ended. I will **not** try again. When you move, send me a change of address; when you go home from school for the summer, send me your home address. There are now about 550 of us. I need all the help I can get to keep things straight.

Speaking of help: Ana Casacuberta comes by twice a month to help me get hte filing under control. By actual count, I received 84 letters last week alone. (I've answered 69 at the time I'm tying this.) The letters are in boxes and piles. ana records the membership information I've scribbled on the envelopes and we put the letters in the file cabinet. Yes, I save all my mail. Thank you, Ana, for allowing me to dream of one day being truly organized.

Queen's Own now has a member in Norway and one in Guam. In last week's mail was a request for information from Italy. We are growing!

We need a new QO button design! All ideas gratefully considered. Many of you draw beautiful Companions, but I'd like something different--something broader. Misty's books are expanding into new territories; I'd like the button design to somehow reflect that.

Some QO members opted to pay me just half their duest back in September and never made a second payment. Their membership ended with the February issue. If the date by your last name on the address label is 3/91, this is your last issue.
