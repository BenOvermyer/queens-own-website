Dear Misty Fans,
</p><p>
<img align="right" alt="flowers" border="0" loading="lazy" src="/qo227.jpg"/>Good news for you Wing Commander fans! The promo from Baen books reads: "One of Baen's Biggest Stars and a Team Leader from Wing Commander Team Up on a Tie-In to the Bestselling Computer Game of all Time!" Yes, it's another collaboration from Misty and Ellen Guon, and it's a Wing Commander book, to be released in time for Christmas.
</p><p>
<i>WING COMMANDER: FREEDOM FLIGHT</i> features Ralgha nar Rhallas, captain of a war cruiser, noble lord of the Kilrathi Empire - and a rebel.... Captain K'Kai of Firekka, first of her winged species to leave her planet for the stars . . . .  Captain Ian St. John, call sign "Hunter," hotshot human pilot - he thinks he's just in it for the hot flying . . . . Rikik, K'Kai's niece, hereditary leader of her clan - all she wants to do is follow in her notorious relative's footsteps and fly the stars . . . . They were as disreputable a gang of misfits as ever flew between the stars. But together they could free a world . . . . WOW!
</p><p>
Tornadoes tore through Oklahoma June 19th. On June 20th Larry posted this on GEnie: "Tornadoes are funny things.</p><p>
"Misty and I came home from Tulsa yesterday, after spending advance money on some rather nice sound equipment, to find my new garage blown apart. It was blown apart with my 'S7 in it. In the light of day, we found something rather fascinating this morning.</p><p>
"We found the path of the tornado that hit the garage cleanly etched out in our field, meandering to the garage only, and hooking behind it to the twenty feet between the Aerie proper and the old garage, where it threw a footlocker of camping gear through the porch railing.</p><p>
"And there the tornado lifted, two feet or so from the Aerie.	Pretty nifty . . . .</p><p>
"Lost two portfolios and an archive file. And a box of uncirculated prints. The good news for you all is that the prints and art you have are now even more collectible. It's a pretty cool planet, all in all....</p><p>
"As disasters go, this isn't one. Rubber, plastic, metal, glass, and enamel is all a car is. Paper, graphite, and acrylic is all art is. And wood and metal is all construction is. We have resources, and are dealing with it, no problem. As far as the portfolios--I can make more art. There are people who are on Welfare who lost their homes... Don't get too upset over this . . . if you want to get upset over something, our agent, Russell Galen--who is also a dear personal friend--just lost a lung this week to a large but benign cancer. Send your good thoughts his way. He deserves an enormous amount of credit from the fans. It has been his work from the beginning that has brought our work to you folks . . . "
</p><p>
The George Alec Effinger Medical Fund announces a new fund raiser, the "WORLDCON-FOR-A-BUCK" raffle. Win a chance to attend WorldCon '93 in San Francisco for only a dollar. The Grand Prize includes airline travel from anywhere in the continental US, WorldCon membership, and 4 nights hotel accommodations and meal allowance.</p><p>
All you have to do to enter is write your name, address and telephone number on as many index cards or 3z5 pieces of paper as you like. Drop them in an envelope, with one dollar for each chance, and mail them to [Ed note 2004:  Out-of-date contact info. withheld.] before September 3rd. Checks are to made payable to The George Alec Effmger Medical Fund. All donations and/or chances are to be sent directly to the NFSFA address.
</p><p>
"NFSFA" stands for "Niagara Falls Science Fiction Association." They're putting in a bid for WorldCon '98 with the slogan "The Falls is Great for '98." Watch for NFSFA members at conventions you attend and offer your support. I think an affordable WorldCon here in the Northeast is a great idea.
</p><p>
Would you like to hear the Irish pub music mentioned in <i>Born to Run</i>? Send for Serrated Edge, a new release from Firebird Arts &amp; Music. The music, Celtic rock, is performed by TEMPEST. The band makes a "guest appearance" in the fourth SERRAted Edge book, <i>Chrome Circle</i>.
</p><p>
Send a #10 SASE for Firebird's catalog of filk, books, collectibles, and other treasures to [Ed note 2004:  Out-of-date contact info. withheld.] . For credit card orders you can call [Ed note 2004:  Out-of-date contact info. withheld.] . For Oregon and international calls, use [Ed note 2004:  Out-of-date contact info. withheld.] .

</p><h2>Convention News:</h2>
<p>
ATTENTION all lucky people in the Dayton, Ohio area: Misty will be doing a book signing at Books and Company this Friday evening, July 24, between 7:30 and 9:30. The store will hand out line numbers starting at 6:30. You may bring already purchased books to be signed (but it's polite to buy something while you're there). The address is [Ed note 2004:  Out-of-date contact info. withheld.] . Books and Company is celebrating science fiction month with 13 % off all SF/F titles. They will take phone orders for autographed copies of Misty's books (accompanied by a credit card number, off course.)	The store hopes to have in stock by this time both the paperback version of <i>Winds of Fate</i>, and the Misty/Anne
McCaffrey collaboration, The Ship Who Searched.	QO member Karen Bertke can give you directions. Call her at [Ed note 2004:  Out-of-date contact info. withheld.] .
</p><p>
RiverCon XVII is July 31-August 2 at the Hyatt Regency in Louisville, Kentucky. Misty is Guest of Honor and lots of QO folk plan to attend. It's a bit late to send for info but you can make a room reservation at the Hyatt by calling [Ed note 2004:  Out-of-date contact info. withheld.] , and membership for the Con is $25 at the door. Hope to see you there!
</p><p>
For you Iowa area residents, Misty and Larry plan to attend ICON October 2-4 in Coralville. For information, write to Icon [Ed note 2004:  Out-of-date contact info. withheld.] .
