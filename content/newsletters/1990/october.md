</p><p>
Dear Misty Fans,
</p><p>
The purpose of the Queen's Own newsletter is to keep you all informed of events in Misty's life--new books, convention appearances, new tapes, etc.  Well, I have a different kind of event to announce this month.  I am very pleased to inform you all that Misty Lackey and Larry Dixon are getting married!  The wedding will be a private ceremony in Las Vegas in December.  Friends and fans are asked to celebrate withthem at the convention Circle Oroboros in Meridian, Mississippi in February.  (Circle O is where Misty and LArry first met.)  The date and address of the con will be in the next newsletter.  Ask Santa Claus for tickets to Mississippi!
</p><p>
This is certainly a "GOOD NEWS" issue.  Baen has asked Misty and Ellen Guon for two more Eric Banyon books.  The titles at this time are <i>Summoned to Tourney</i> and <i>Leagues Beyond</i>.
</p><p>
Another Valdemar series from DAW!  The focus is on Elspeth in the trilogy, <i>The Mage Winds</i>.  The books are <i>Winds of Fate</i>, <i>Winds of Change</i>, and <i>Winds of Fury</i>.  And it will be in hard cover!
</p><p>
Tor has expressed an interest in more Diane Tregarde books.
</p><p>
Misty's story in <i>Marion Zimmer Bradley's Fantasy Magazine</i>, Issue 10, will star Diana and Andre.  You can order just that one issue of the magazine [Ed. note 2002:  Out-of-date information withheld.].
</p><p>
More from Misty:
</p><p>
"I suppose that in everything I write I try to expound the 'creed' I gave my character Di tRegarde in <i>Burning Water</i>--there's no such thing as 'one, true way;' the only answers worth having are the ones you find for yourself; leave the world better than you found it; if it isn't true, going to spread a little love around, or going to do some good, don't say it, do it, or think it.  And the last--and lonvest--there are only three things worth living for:  love, freedom, and the chance to do some good; they're the same things worth dying for, and if you aren't willing to die for the things worth living for, you might as well turn in your membership in the human race.  Maybe that's a bit simplistic, but--there it is.  I'd like to think that in what I write I'm living up to that."
</p><p>
And that is how you earn your Herald whites in the "real" world!
</p><p>
The contract has been signed for the collaboration between Misty and Anne McCaffrey.  I'll tell you more about it when I have more details.
</p><p>
Firebird will soon be featuring more Misty-related merchandise.  If you're not on their mailing list, send your address to [Ed. note 2002:  Out-of-date information withheld.  Firebird's website can be found at <a href="http://www.firebirdarts.com">http://www.firebirdarts.com</a>.].
</p><p>

That means on a narrow strip of paper that will fit within the right hand margin. I've received a number of lovely ideas that have been either too short or too wide. [Ed. note 2002:  Current art may be sent to <a href="mailto:Legend@malcor.com">Legend@malcor.com</a>.].

I've enjoyed every copy of <i>Arrow's Message</i>, our bi-monthly zine.  [Ed. note 2002:  Out-of-date information withheld.].

Help Vicki McElfresh get her <i>Companion's Rest</i> zine off to a flying start.  [Ed. note 2002:  Out-of-date information withheld.].

If you would like to buy a zine or submit art, poetry, or a story, send a SASE for price information or guidelines to Nathaniel, Stephanie, or Vicki--or to all three!

There are coffee mugs featuring the "For Companionship choose Valdemar" design available now from Tess Wade.  [Ed. note 2002:  Out-of-date information withheld.].

It's available in gold or silver, in sizes 5, 6, or 7.  Write to the museum [Ed. note 2002:  Out-of-date information withheld.]..  And thanks to Ambria Ridenow for sharing the information.

</p><h3>Hello from Herald Housemother:</h3>
<p>
Let's see . . . For those of you who keep score, besides my usual "creative" spellings and zip codes, I've scrambled titles in a list of Misty's short stories, and I've assigned the wrong gifts to a Herald.  What mistakes will Herald Housemother make in the next issue?  You'll have to wait until the middle of December to find out, there is no November mailing.
</p><p>
If you wish to send a holiday card to Misty and Larry, [Ed. note 2002:  Out-of-date information withheld.  Fan mail may be sent to Misty and Larry at the High Flight address, listed below.]  Please leave froom for the forwarding address.  Please mail before December 15th.
</p><p>
Some of you who are attending SoonerCon have expressed your delight at the prospect of meeting me.  I am bright, charming, and witty for three--maybe even five--minutes a day.  If you miss me during those few minutes, you will be stuck with a tired and cranky old lady.  Please don't expect too much!
</p><p>
<b>Questions and Answers:</b>
</p><p>
<b>Why do you spell magick with a "k"?</b>  That distinguishes the mysteries of the universe and the imagination from the stage magic of Houdini and David Copperfield.
</p><p>
<b>Which is correc, "a" SASE or "an" SASE?</b> I use "a" because I pronounce SASE as a sone syllable word.  Others prefer "an" because they are more comfortable spelling out the word as four separate letters.  No matter how you pronounce it, be sure to include it!  Most fan-related activities are very not-for-profit.
</p><p>
<b>How quickly should pen pal letters be answered?</b> Mail should be answered as quickly as time and your life style permit.  Six days seems reasonable; six weeks seems to be my pattern.  Sometimes that stretches out to six months.
</p><p>
<b>Why do you end the newsletter with a pun?</b> Are you kidding?  Have you read "An Object Lesson" in <i>Domains of Darkover</i>?  Misty started it!
</p><p>

</p><p>
Welcome, <b>Herald Gwendolyn Katran</b>, chosen by <b>Kandaron</b>, and with the gifts of Fetching, Farsight and Mindspeech.  <i>Kathleen Wing</i> sent her persona, sketches of Gwen, her twin sisters and Herald mother, a story, a song, and a poem.
</p><p>
Welcome, <b>Herald Kristia Mahaliah Kolnmen</b>, chosen by the Companion <b>Chandra</b>.  <i>Kris Kovarovic</i> tells us Krissa's gifts are Fetching, Mindspeech, and Animal Mindspeech.  Kris sent her persona and that of a friend, a list of Krissa's likes and dislikes, her travel diary, three poems, a song (music and lyrics), and pictures of her characters in different costumes.
</p><p>
Welcome, <b>Herald Toris Mikel D'Havn</b> and <b>Bard Tishara Mishel D'Havn</b>.  <i>Dana Jeanne Norris</i> sent detailed character studies of the twins Toris and Tishara and their friend Valley Rohrer, two stories, a map and a recipe.

Welcome, <b>Herald Brandt Kristofferson</b>, chosen by <b>Tarissa</b>, and gifted with Foresight, Fetching, and a touch of Mindspeech and Empathy.  <i>Rob Von Allmen</i> sent the information on his persona in the form of introductory letters.  He included two short stories and two poems.

Welcome, <b>Herald Cheshire Marenn McRase</b>, chosen by the mare <b>Anschad</b>.  <i>Katy Stahl</i> tells us Ches's gifts are strong Mindspeech and a smidgeon of Firestarting.
