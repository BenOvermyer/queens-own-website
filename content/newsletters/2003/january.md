Herald Alora writes to tell us about a great website ([http://www.elfwood.com](http://www.elfwood.com)) that she found:

"Elfwood is a worldwide home to Fantasy/Sci-Fi art and literature, which has grown immensely since its humble beginnings in May of 1996. This nonprofit site is dedicated to publishing works by artists in the areas of fantasy, sci-fi, fandom, and written works (stories and poetry of related themes). The site also offers How-To Guides, an online newsletter, forums, and other links.

"Allowing all fantasy artists and writers to show their works to the general public, Elfwood helps them gain a reputation and critiques, creative and supportive comments, and contacts. (Copyright and all rights therein are maintained by the artists and writers.)

"Elfwoods goal is to continue working with amateurs and pros, in the field of art and fiction, while giving numerous new artists an excellent opportunity to publish their material; while ever expanding their site.

"I have an art gallery of my own in Elfwood, and I've found it a wonderfully motivational and inspiring community for artists, writers, and everyone who enjoys the boundless world of fantasy, myths, creations, and dreams … I recommend this site highly!"

Please note that, according to the Elfwood rules, "Your artwork and/or writing must be 100% your own work. If the art/writing is not fully yours, from idea to finished creation, then don't put it up HERE." Please note that **no public posting of Mercedes Lackey fanfiction is allowed on the Internet**. (This includes talking in character in chatrooms or sending stories via e-mail. The only exception is that there are approved QO RPG events, and we will notify you when and where those will occur.) You can submit fanfiction to and read it in the various approved Mercedes Lackey fanzines. The approved fanzines are listed at:

[Fan fiction archive](/fan-fiction/)

You can also find contact information below in this newsletter. Be aware that while most of these publications are still going strong, some are up for adoption and others have discontinued publication. We try to keep our contact information as up to date as possible, but sometimes things do not get changed as quickly as we would like.

Also note that every time you write a story based on Misty's stories, you need to file a release form with High Flight. (Releases are not required for art or poetry.) You can find the release form at:

[Release form instructions](/release-form-instructions/)

Please support all of our fanzines. Our editors need as much fanfiction, artwork, and other materials as you can send them &lt;g>. The Queen's Own fanzines are some of the best we've ever seen in fandom, and we're very proud of them. Please, subscribe and contribute to them.

Instead of an interview this month, we have a brief article from Queen's Own member Herald Alora about her business. You've seen her art in many of our newsletters and Queen's Own fanzines. In fact, she just won last year's submission contest for the person who sent the most art or stories to our QO zines. Herald Alora's work is extraordinary. Do check out what she has to offer!

Zhai'helleva and Happy New Year,

Herald-Mage Adept Danya Winterborn and Healer Adept Moonstar
