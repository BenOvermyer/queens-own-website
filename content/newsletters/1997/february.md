</p><p>
<img align="right" alt="Border Art" border="0" loading="lazy" src="/qo289.jpg"/>
<i>The Faces of Fantasy</i> is a collection of photographs of fantasy writers taken by Patti Perret.  Each author has a full page picture and a chance to say a few words.  There are photos of Marion Zimmer Bradley, Anne McCaffrey, Peter Beagle, Storm Constantine, Barbara Hambly, Emma Bull, Charles de Lint, Melanie Rawn and many, many more. Misty's photo shows her holding 'Kaika, her umbrella cockatoo.  Her write-up speaks of the importance of "paying forward.".  She and Larry feel that htere is no way for anyone to ever pay back all the good things that come along, so the best response is to pay <i>forward</i>, to give of oneself when needed.  This philosophy is reflected in their writing and in their care of wild birds.
</p><p>
<i>The Faces of Fantasy</i> is a large size paperback published by Tor and priced at [Ed. note 2005:  Out-of-date info withheld.].  For information of Tor Books on the World Wide Web, use [Ed. note 2005:  Out-of-date info withheld.].  (Buy <i>Faces</i> before Dragon*Con and play spot the author.)
</p><p>
The latest issue of <i>Marion Zimmer Bradley's FANTASY Magazine</i>, #34,has the usual excellent short stories, an interview with Ellen Kushner, and two very informative articles.  Marion's "Why Did My Story Get Regected?" and Heather Rose Jones' "Writers Talk Back: Show Me" are filled with helpful advice for would-be writers.  Subscribe to <i>Marion Zimmer Bradley's FANTASY Magazine</i> for [Ed. note 2005:  Out-of-date info withheld.].  Make your check payable to Marion Zimmer Bradley and mail your order to [Ed. note 2005:  Out-of-date info withheld.].

<i>Sword of Ice and Other Tales of Valdemar</i> are worth waiting for, don't you agree?  I especially enjoyed the stories co-authored by Misty and Philip "Sarge" Austin.  Sarge was one of our earliest QO members, and wrote regularly for Arrow's Message, our first zine.
</p><p>
Elizabeth Moon has a new hard cover coming next month, <i>Once a Hero</i>.  Her <i>Remnant Population</i> is due out then in paperback.  Good news for all you Anne McCaffrey fans: for the first time in three years there's a new Pern novel, <i>Dragonseye</i>.  Check your bookstores.

<b>Firebird</b> is adding more new products.  Soon you'll be able to purchase charted cross-stitch designs for the Valdemar crest, Fairgrove logo, a gryphon and more. (Findings are hard-to-find bits and piece that add the finishing touch.)

</p><h3>From Herald Housemother:</h3>
<p>
In <i>Burning Water</i> Misty's character Diana Tregarde says, "If it isn't true, going to do some good, or spread a little love around, don't say it, do it, or think it."  This positive philodophy may express Misty's point of view, but that doesn't make her Diana Tregarde.  There are those among Misty's readers who insist on confusing Misty wtih her characters and they react accordingly, going so far as to threaten her life because they disagree with a character's actions.  Do these smae lunatics think Stephen King is a rabid dog?  a possessed car?  a haunted hotel?  As Misty wrote in the Author's Note in <i>Sacred Ground</i>, "I am not a guru, shaman, Grand High Pooh-Bah, Guardian, Mistress of the Martian Arts, Avatar, Cosmic Earth Mother, or any incarnation of the same . . . To confuse me with what I write is as fallacious as confusing a truck driver with his Peterbilt."  In spite of this disclaimer, the lunatics persist.
</p><p>
The latest threats are under investigation.  Why are the ravings of a probable lunatic being taken so seriously?  Because Misty and Larry are having nightmares, not only about the danger to themselves, but the danger to us, their fans and friends.  Imagine an attack at a convention or a book signing . . .
</p><p>
Expected mailing dates for the rest of 1997 are 4/5, 5/17, 6/21, 8/2, 9/13, 10/25, and 12/6.  Mailing Day Helpers needed!  Come for the weekend if you wish; we have a spare bedroom.  Visit beautiful South Jersey and pet the neighbor's burros and goats (and our cats).  Of course you won't get paid, but we will feed you well.
