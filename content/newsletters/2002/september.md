This month�s interview is with Herald Laliea Shathgran (Toni McQuilken).  If you would like to be interviewed for the QO newsletter, please send Danya (<a href="mailto:Legend@malcor.com">Legend@malcor.com</a>) your answers to the questions listed below.  (If you are a fanzine or newsletter editor who has not yet been interviewed, please let Danya know.  There is a separate set of questions for editors.)
</p><p>
Zhai'helleva,
</p><p>
Herald-Mage Adept Danya Winterborn and Healer Adept Moonstar
</p><p>
</p><ol>
<li><b>When did you join Queen's Own?</b>
<p>
August 2001.
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>What got you hooked on Misty's books?</b>
<p>
I ran across one in the book store, and as I was looking for a new fantasy series I bought it (It was the Gryphon series.).  After those, I was hooked and I now own every Velgarth novel she has put out including the new <i>Valdemar Companion</i> and the short story books.
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>What else do you read?</b>
<p>
Just about everything. Literature like Shakespeare, fiction, mystery, fantasy . . . a better question would be what do I not read.
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>What types of music do you listen to?</b>
<p>
Again, there is not much I don't like.  I think rap and heavy metal are the only two I don't have in my collection.  I just recently discovered Misty's filk, so I am in the process of finding that right now.
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>What movies do you enjoy?</b>
<p>
Comedy is my favorite.  If I am going to sit still for that long, I want to be amused.
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>What was the first con you went to?</b>
<p>
Sadly to say, I have yet to attend any.  No time and none close by.
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>Why did you get involved in fandom? </b>
<p>
I wanted to learn more about the books and interact with people who love them as much as I do.
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>What do you like best about fandom? </b>
<p>
The community.  This is the only place I can say something like "I wish my Mindspeech was stronger," or "When will we find out more about Alberich" and not get funny looks.
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>What other things besides Queen's Own occupy your time?</b>
<p>
Well, work mostly.  I am an Assistant Editor at a magazine.  Other than that, I read, cross-stitch (If anyone knows where I can get some Valdemar-related projects, let me know!) and hang out with friends.
</p><p>
[Ed. note:  Misty did a couple of patterns for old newsletters.  I�m not sure if we can get them to scan, but we�ll work on it.]
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>What types of things would you like to see Queen's Own do in the coming years?</b>
<p>
Well, I am looking forward to the cookbook . . . Other than that, maybe have live chats with ML if she is willing.
</p></li></ol>
