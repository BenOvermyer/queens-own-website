<li><b>When did you join Queen's Own?</b>
<p>
I didn't:  I started it!  Our first newsletter was 1 page, sent to people who had written to DAW, Misty, or Terri Lee at Firebird in February 1988.
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>What got you hooked on Misty's books?</b>
<p>
Her characters.  I identified with Talia; I became Talia while reading Arrows.  No other author had gotten me so emotionally involved.
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>What else do you read?</b>
<p>
Sci-fi, mysteries . . . I used to read horror but today's headlines are horrible enough so I've switched to romances; I like the assurance of a happy ending.
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>What types of music do you listen to?</b>
<p>
I love classical, but I gotta admit I love country and western too. Also ethnic, filk, big bands . . . it depends on my mood.
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>What movies do you enjoy?</b>
<p>
I saw <i>Shrek</i>, <i>The Mummy Returns</i> and <i>The Lord of the Rings</i> and enjoyed them immensely. Most of my movie watching is the classics on TV.
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>What was the first con you went to?</b>
<p>
My first con was in Jackson MS (Springfest? 1989? I can't remember), and I went to meet Misty.
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>Why did you get involved in fandom?</b>
<p>
I needed some fun in my life, and I wanted to have friends who could see the value of imagination and of being silly.
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>What do you like best about fandom?</b>
<p>
Fandom is a great equalizer. Age, economic background, even geographic location are unimportant. It's the shared interest that brings us together.
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>What other things besides Queen's Own occupy your time?</b>
<p>
At the time I ran QO, nothing!  My family was very tolerant of my "hobby."  Now I am Grammy, not Herald-Housemother, and I can't wait until the grandkids are old enough to enjoy Misty's worlds.
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>What types of things would you like to see Queen�s Own do in the coming years?</b>
<p>
Ah, that's for you young folks to decide. I would like to see fandom in general return to the goal of having fun and being an outlet for creativity.  Fandom is NOT a way of life; it's a way of play. All intelligent species need play!
</p><p>
