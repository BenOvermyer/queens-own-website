**Holiday Greetings from Highflight:**

"Hello to everyone from Highflight Towers.

"We've had a rather hectic month, which means that Misty's usual Christmas message has become rather delayed. Apologies for that. We've all had gastric flu, and with Sanctuary becoming precipitously due, Misty herself is working away furiously so you all have the finale to the trilogy to read ASAP!

"On behalf of Misty and Larry then, it falls to me, the captive Brit (as Misty nicknamed me), to write you all a short note. First up, and most important: A very happy holidays and a joyful New Year to each and every one of you. We here are very lucky to have a fan base that is so friendly, upbeat and a delight to be in touch with. Your good wishes keep our spirits up in the rough patches (e.g. the dreaded flu bug!).

"Misty and Larry are both looking into new projects, but continuations of previous series continue apace. Misty's *The Fairy Godmother* was just released into paperback, so she has been visiting the forums online at [http://www.luna-books.com](http://www.luna-books.com) whenever she has a spare moment available. You may consider joining the luna-tics there! Luna does try to get the author of this month's released book to communicate with the fans there, and it seems to work well. We have to say, a big congratulations to Luna on their first successful year. May there be many more.

"*Sanctuary* is due out in March, and I personally am looking forward to it, having loved the previous two. Misty and James Mallory are already in cahoots over the third book in the Obsidian trilogy and quite possibly more in the same universe. This is all a little tentative, pending the completion of the first trilogy of course; but ideas are being bounced around. The third book in the Heirs of Alexandria series is in the pipeline, with Eric Flint and Dave Freer and of course, the delightful Rosemary Edghill is working with Misty on yet more bedlam for your delectation this summer.

"Speaking of bedlam, since the origin of that word was originally �Bethlehem� (Go look it up!), the Christmas holiday season is fairly chaotic for everyone, and that's true for us, too. We all have loved ones across the nation or even around the world. We are very lucky that it is now possible to communicate across those vast distances easier than ever it was before. The fact you are reading this could be taken as an example. The fact you want me to quit rambling suggests I ought to stop, too!

"When more news becomes available, I'm sure Danya will be delighted to help it to your door. Thank you to the volunteers that work tirelessly to make the Queen's Own newsletter what it is. It's much appreciated from this end.

"Once again then, from everyone here have a wonderful holiday and may 2005 be stronger and better for all of us, as nations and as a world.

"Paul"
