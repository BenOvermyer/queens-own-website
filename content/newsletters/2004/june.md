Heyla, Misty fans!

Is everyone ready for summer?! Lots of exciting stuff is happening in the movie and publication worlds. **Zine News:** One of the original QO zines is also coming back. Look for the details on that in the next newsletter as well.

**Persona Updates:** We have several personae pending approval. We hope to get to them very soon. (Cover art for all is up at [Amazon.com](https://dragonlords.fans/bkmlot.htm).)

Coming in October 2004 are the paperback versions of *Exile�s Valor* and *Phoenix and Ashes*.
