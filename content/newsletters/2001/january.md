Heyla, Misty fans!

Happy New Year, everyone!

Queen's Own is charging into 2001 with a new interview series. This time we are talking with the artists who create the beautiful works that you see in the Queen's Own fanzines and newsletters. Some of these folks are professionals; others are strictly amateurs. Our first interview is with Adam King of Great Falls, Montana. Many thanks to Adam and all of the other talented people who are taking time to participate in this series.

Keep up to date with happenings in Queen's Own chapters around the world by joining the Queen's Own chat list. Also remember to visit our private Delphi Forum. Activities range from local meetings at bookstores/coffee houses to discuss Misty's works to a possible Queen's Own Renaissance Faire trip in April/May/June of this year. We're also having people express interest in Queen's Own Midsummer festivals. If you have a QO chapter that wants to sponsor such an activity, please let us know! We'd love to get fans from all over the world involved in these and other events. (Anyone interested in getting Queen's Own sections back in some of the cons?) Also, if any of you are visiting Disneyland or a similar attraction in Southern California, those places are not too far from where Danya lives and from where Moonstar will be living come June. Drop us a line, and we'll try to set up a Queen's Own event so we can meet you face to face!

Thanks for all of your excellent ideas about directions that we can take the club. You are a creative bunch of people, and we're very excited about continuing to work with you in 2001.

**Herald-Mage Master Danya Winterborn** and **Healer Adept Moonstar**

<img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/barcomp.gif"/>

1. **When did you join Queen's Own?**
  September 1995.
2. **What got you hooked on Misty's books?**
  Kendra [Healer Adept Moonstar] bought me the Last Herald Mage Trilogy.
3. **What else do you read?**
  Robert Jordan, Piers Anthony, Roger Zelazny.
4. **What types of music do you listen to?**
  Everything. Mostly heavy metal.
5. **What was the first con you went to?**
  Never been to one.
6. **Do you sell your work at cons?**
  Like I said, never been to one, and I doubt I'd get much for my cheesy sketches.
7. **Who or what inspires your art?**
  Lizards. Dragons and other lizard variants are all I can draw decently. I like to put them in human poses.
8. **Do you have any formal training or mentors?**
  Nope.
9. **Describe the process you go through when you create a piece of art.**
  I see it in my head, and I attempt to get it to look similar on paper.
10. **What other projects besides Queen's Own occupy your time?**
  Dragonlords ([http://www.dragonlordsnet.com](https://dragonlords.fans/index.html)), and I work for a web design company.
