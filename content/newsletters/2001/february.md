<p>
Heyla, Misty fans!
</p><p>
Mercedes Lackey has been busy, busy, busy!  By now many of you have figured out that Misty's <i>Beyond World's End</i>, the next installment in the <i>Bedlam's Bard</i> series is available.  We'll have a review of it in the next issue of <i>The Compass Rose</i>, QO's nonfiction journal, which will hopefully be coded and online in the near future (Young babies don't allow mamas much time for things like coding &lt;g&gt;.)  The paperback edition of <i>The Fire Rose</i> is due out any day.  <i>The Serpent's Shadow</i>, which takes place in the same setting as <i>The Fire Rose</i>, is scheduled for release in March, and look for <i>Elvenborn</i>, the next in the series that Misty is writing with Andre Norton, in April.  Misty is also working on an eight-book juvenile series, inspired by the Harry Potter books, which will follow the adventures of a Herald Trainee through the Collegium.
</p><p>
We are delighted to announce the formation of the Herald's Waystation, the newest chapter of Queen's Own!  Heralds Auralynn and Enyo are heading up this venture.  Check out the details in the "Local Chapters" and "Zines and Newsletters" sections below.
</p><p>
We are very excited about all of the things going on in Queen's Own. Join the QO chatlist or visit the private QO Delphi forum for more details on Renaissance Faire trips, Midsummer get-togethers and a host of other fun things that are in the works.  We already mentioned that the next issue of <i>The Compass Rose</i> will be posted soon, and Issue 2 of <i>Children of Velgarth</i>, the QO 'zine, will be available shortly as well.
</p><p>
This newsletter features our chat with artist Michelle Cornett.  Thanks to all of the talented folk who are participating in this series!  Subscribe to the various QO 'zines (listed below) to see their work as well as art by other QO fans.
</p><p>
Zhai'helleva,
</p><p>
<b>Herald-Mage Master Danya Winterborn</b> and <b>Healer Adept Moonstar</b>
</p><p>
</p><center><img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/barcomp.gif"/></center>
<p>
</p><ol>
<li><b>When did you join Queen's Own?</b>
<p>
1992, I think.
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>What got you hooked on Misty's books?</b>
<p>
The characters, mainly.  They're vivid people, and it can hook you in no time :-).
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>What else do you read?</b>
<p>
I enjoy a good vampire novel, but it's mostly fantasy that I read, like Piers Anthony, Terry Brooks, and Graham Masterson.
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>What types of music do you listen to?</b>
<p>
I'm pretty eclectic when it comes to music.  I listen to anything from Slipknot to Loreena McKennit, Enya to Green Day.
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>What was the first con you went to?</b>
<p>
Never been to one YET, but I'm going to try to attend one in late August, called Con'Fur'ence.
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>Do you sell your work at cons?</b>
<p>
I've been asked to send in some of my pieces, but it never seems like I can find the time or money to do it of yet.  But I hope to!
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>Who or what inspires your art?</b>
<p>
Robin Woods is wonderful.  Music can also stir the creativity juices.
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>Do you have any formal training or mentors?</b>
<p>
I'm completely self-taught.  I remember in high school my art teacher getting onto me for helping other classmates 'cuz she can always tell its me by my own unique way of drawing.
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>Describe the process you go through when you create a piece of art.</b>
<p>
Oye!  I never know what I'm drawing in the beginning.  I usually just start with basic body outline and go from there.  People think I'm strange when they ask what I'm drawing and I can only say "I'll know when I'm finished." Hehe . . .
</p><p>
</p></li><li><b>What other projects besides Queen's Own occupy your time?</b>
<p>
I use to be an avid contributor to Karen Bertke's <i>The Herald's Companion</i> and Patricia Roe's <i>The Unicorn Vale</i>. . . as well as the past fanzine called <i>Betes Noires</i> dedicated to vampires, werewolves and other creatures of fantasy and horror . . . You could also find some of my work in past issues of <i>Companions' Grove</i> . . . I always look for others that I enjoy sending my work to.
</p></li></ol>
