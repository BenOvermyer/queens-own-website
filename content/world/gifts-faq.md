</font><center><h1>Gifts FAQ</h1></center>
<center><h2>(Revised 2/7/02)</h2></center>
<p>
People often ask what characters with the various Gifts are capable of doing.  Here are the known Gifts and what they do:
</p><p>
</p><ul>
<li>Animal Mindspeech (telepathy with animals)
</li><li>Doublesight (ability of a Healer to see distress or pain inside a patient)
</li><li>Earth Magic (also called Earth-Witchery; see below)
</li><li>Earth-sense
</li><li>Empathy (also called Feeling)
</li><li>FarSight (also called FarSeeing, Looking and Seeing; clairvoyance)
</li><li>Fetching (teleportation)
</li><li>Firestarting (a form of Fetching that calls fire from the elemental plane)
</li><li>Foresight
</li><li>Healing
</li><li>Mage Gift (the ability to sense the energy around living things and to use that energy to work spells)
</li><li>Mindspeech (also called Thoughtsensing and FarSpeaking; telepathy with Humans)
</li></ul>
<p>
Although sometimes mentioned as a Gift, Mind Healing and Trauma Healing are actually learned  Healer specialties.
</p><p>
Mindspeech has a "Private Mode", which can be used so that mental conversations cannot be overheard by other Mindspeakers.
</p><p>
</p><h2>Earth Magic</h2>
<p>
Earth Magic (also called Earth-Witchery) is not true magic (which is also called "High Magic").  For information about true magic, see <a href="/magic-of-velgarth/">The Magic of Velgarth</a>.  Earth Magic is the low form of magic used by Hedge-Wizards and Earth-Witches.   The power can appear as a Mind Magic Gift as well.
</p><p>
Earth mages can work minor spells.  These mages are also called Earth-Witches and Wisewomen, partially depending on the cultural background of the person who uses the term.
</p><p>
Most Earth Magic is tied to the state of the land and is used to heal land that has been damaged by magic (as well as by nonmagical means).
</p><p>
