</font><center><h1>Travel Guide FAQ</h1></center>
<center><h2>(Revised 10/18/20)</h2></center>
<p>
Here we briefly survey a number of types of places that people ask about on a regular basis.  For geography, you really want to refer to the maps by Larry Dixon that are printed in the front of many of Misty's Velgarth novels.  There is also a partial map in <i>The Valdemar Companion</i> (pp. 205-210).  (We are told that efforts are underway to make the entire map of the known realms of Velgarth available as a poster.  Watch the QO <a href="/newsletter-guidance/">newsletter</a> for details.)  Kerrie Hughes has written a delightful piece, "A Travelers' Guide to Valdemar and the Surrounding Kingdoms", in <i>The Valdemar Companion</i> (pp. 81-108).  We highly recommend it to anyone who is writing fanfic about characters travelling on Velgarth.
</p><p>

</p><h2>Cities</h2>
<p>
There are very few settlements large enough to call cities on Velgarth, but here are the ones we know about and the countries where they are located:
</p><p>
</p><ul>
<li>Delton (Ruvan)
</li><li>Haven (Valdemar; capitol)
</li><li>Highjorune (capitol; Lineas)
</li><li>Oberdorn (Ruvan)
</li></ul>
<p>
</p><h2>Towns</h2>
<p>
Most of the towns mentioned in the books are shown on the map Larry Dixon did with Daniel Green for <i>The Valdemar Companion</i> (pp. 205-210).  Here are some other settlements that are mentioned in the concordance:
</p><p>
</p><ul>
<li>Berrybay (Valdemar)
</li><li>Errold's Grove (Valdemar)
</li><li>Felwether
</li><li>Fromish (Jkatha)
</li><li>Great Harsey (Rethwellan)
</li><li>Greenhaven (Valdemar)
</li><li>Hevenbeck (Valdemar)
</li><li>Hielmarsh (Rethwellan)
</li><li>Kelmskeep (Valdemar)
</li><li>Kettlesmith (Valdemar)
</li><li>Lyavor (Ruvan)
</li><li>Lythecare (Jkatha)
</li><li>Vidan (Ruvan)
</li><li>Waymeet (Valdemar)
</li><li>Westmark (Valdemar)
</li></ul>
<p>
Towns are run by a Mayor and a Town Council.  Each town has its own gaol.
</p><p>
</p><h2>Villages and Smaller Settlements</h2>
<p>
These places are smaller than towns.
</p><p>
</p><ul>
<li>Covia (under k'Treva protection)
</li><li>Delcare (Valdemar, seven days from Haven by Companion)
</li><li>Garthhold (under k'Treva protection)
</li><li>Hunsted (Guard Outpost; Hardorn Border)
</li></ul>
<p>
Villages are run by Mayors.
</p><p>

</li><li>Fivepenny (tavern; Haven)
</li><li>Griffen's Egg (tavern; Haven)
</li><li>Halfway Inn (Valdemar)
</li><li>Hanging Goose Tavern (Hardorn)

</li><li>Hostel at Keeper's Crossing (Hardorn)
</li><li>Inn of the Green Man (Highjorune, Lineas)
</li><li>King's Arms (tavern in Haven)
</li><li>North Road Inn (Valdemar)
</li><li>Owl Inn (Valdemar)
</li><li>The Page Inn (Highjorune, Lineas)
</li><li>The Virgin and Stars (tavern; Haven, third year Bardic students)

<p>
</p><h2>Ranks</h2>
<p>
There are ranks within the populations of the various countries.  Here are the ones we know about:
</p><p>

<p>
</p><ul>
<li>Monarch (King; no queens have been mentioned)
</li><li>Town Councillor
</li></ul>
<p>
</p><h3>Karse</h3>
<p>
</p><ul>
<li>Son of the Sun (Ruler; always male until Solaris, who is female)
</li></ul>
<p>

<p>
</p><ul>
<li>Monarch (King or Queen)
</li><li>Heir to the Throne
</li><li>Lord Marshall

</li><li>Armsmaster
</li></ul>
