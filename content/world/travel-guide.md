## Travel Guide FAQ

Here we briefly survey a number of types of places that people ask about on a regular basis. For geography, you really want to refer to the maps by Larry Dixon that are printed in the front of many of Misty's Velgarth novels. There is also a partial map in *The Valdemar Companion* (pp. 205-210). (We are told that efforts are underway to make the entire map of the known realms of Velgarth available as a poster. Watch the QO [newsletter](/newsletter-guidance/) for details.) Kerrie Hughes has written a delightful piece, "A Travelers' Guide to Valdemar and the Surrounding Kingdoms", in *The Valdemar Companion* (pp. 81-108). We highly recommend it to anyone who is writing fanfic about characters travelling on Velgarth.

## Cities

There are very few settlements large enough to call cities on Velgarth, but here are the ones we know about and the countries where they are located:

- Delton (Ruvan)
- Haven (Valdemar; capitol)
- Highjorune (capitol; Lineas)
- Oberdorn (Ruvan)

## Towns

Most of the towns mentioned in the books are shown on the map Larry Dixon did with Daniel Green for *The Valdemar Companion* (pp. 205-210). Here are some other settlements that are mentioned in the concordance:

- Berrybay (Valdemar)
- Errold's Grove (Valdemar)
- Felwether
- Fromish (Jkatha)
- Great Harsey (Rethwellan)
- Greenhaven (Valdemar)
- Hevenbeck (Valdemar)
- Hielmarsh (Rethwellan)
- Kelmskeep (Valdemar)
- Kettlesmith (Valdemar)
- Lyavor (Ruvan)
- Lythecare (Jkatha)
- Vidan (Ruvan)
- Waymeet (Valdemar)
- Westmark (Valdemar)

Towns are run by a Mayor and a Town Council. Each town has its own gaol.

## Villages and Smaller Settlements

These places are smaller than towns.

- Covia (under k'Treva protection)
- Delcare (Valdemar, seven days from Haven by Companion)
- Garthhold (under k'Treva protection)
- Hunsted (Guard Outpost; Hardorn Border)

Villages are run by Mayors.

## Ranks

There are ranks within the populations of the various countries. Here are the ones we know about:

### Hardorn

- Monarch (King; no queens have been mentioned)
- Town Councillor

### Karse

- Son of the Sun (Ruler; always male until Solaris, who is female)

### Valdemar

- Monarch (King or Queen)
- Heir to the Throne
- Lord Marshall
- Lord Seneschal
- Lord or Lady
- Armsmaster

## Cultures

Misty has created many of her cultures by combining elements of various Terran cultures. Here are some that we know about:

- Haighlei (Great African Empires and Japanese)
- Karsite (medieval German, Moslem and Babylonian)
- Shin'a'in (Plains Indian and Mongol)
