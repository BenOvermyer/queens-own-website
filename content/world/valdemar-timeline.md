## Chronological Order

Here are all of the Valdemar novels in the order in which their events take
place. Dates are given in the "After the Founding" (AF) reckoning used in the
books; works set before the founding of Valdemar are marked "BF" (Before the
Founding). The timeline is adapted from the chart that appears at the front of
the Valdemar books, extended to cover the novels published since. A date marked
"c." is approximate, and the exact years within a reign vary from source to
source.

Two further Valdemar novels have been announced but not yet published:
*Gryphon's Valor*, the second book of Kelvren's Saga, and *Vanyel and Vixen*,
which is set between *Magic's Promise* and *Magic's Price*.

*Sword of Ice* does not fall neatly onto the timeline. It contains tales that stretch from just before Vanyel's time (Savil's first encounter with the Tayledras) up through Selenay's reign. The short stories gathered in *Oathblood* follow Tarma and Kethry across the reigns listed above, and "A Herald's Journey" in *The Valdemar Companion* is also set during Selenay's reign.
