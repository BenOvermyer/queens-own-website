</p><p><i>Phoenyx and Ashes</i> (from the Elemental Mage series) is slated for an October 2004 release, as is <i>Sanctuary</i> (the latter of which is the sequel to <i>Joust</i> and <i>Alta</i>).

</p><p>
<i>Shadow Mountain</i> is the first book of the new Fantasy Trilogy with James Mallory. This has all the High Fantasy trimmings and should appeal to the Robert Jordan audience.
</p><p>
Other items in the works include Elvenbred, A Circus Of Witches, Queen of Air &amp; Darkness, <i>To Light A Candle</i> (with James Mallory; the sequel to Shadow Mountain), <i>Ill Met By Moonlight</i> (with Roberta Gellis; the sequel to This Scepter'd Isle), <i>Much Fall Of Blood</i> (with Flint and Freer; sequel to This Rough Magic), and Music To My Sorrow (with Rosemary Edgehill; the sequel to Mad Maudlin).
