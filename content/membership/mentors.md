<p>
Joining Queen's Own is the first thing many of our members have done by way of participatiing in fandom.  This means that a lot of people have a lot of questions that are not necessarily related to Queen's Own.  So in 1991, Queen's Own started the Mentor Program to help field some of these questions.  If you need information on QO-related topics, e-mail Herald-Mage Adept Danya Winterborn at <a href="mailto:Legend@malcor.com">Legend@malcor.com</a>.  If you want information on some other area of fandom, however, our mentors have graciously volunteered to answer questions in their fields of expertise.

</p><h2>How to Volunteer</h2>
<p>
Any of you experienced folk have time to help out the newbies?  We need mentors to cover filk, Cons, zines and other fannish activities to volunteer to share their enthusiasms with new members--who may have no idea of what we're talking about!  If you feel you are mentor material, contact Herald-Mage Adept Danya Winterborn at <a href="mailto:Legend@malcor.com">Legend@malcor.com</a>.  As Judith pointed out in the <a href="/newsletters/1990/july/">July 1990 Queen's Own Newsletter</a>, "There is a wide, weird, and wonderful world of fannish activities going on; let's share our knowledge so we can all play."
