You may browse the site and use the downloads provided for reading. The original site described permission to keep one electronic copy and one printed copy for household use. That statement should not be read as a grant of rights that the club does not own or control. Unless a relevant license or permission allows it, obtain authorization from the rights holder before republishing, distributing, adapting, or using protected material commercially. Giving credit alone does not supply permission. Book-cover images may involve rights held by artists, publishers, or others; appearing on this site or linking to a bookstore does not authorize their reuse elsewhere.

The original site credits L. A. Malcor with creating or customizing some of its buttons. Contact the relevant creator or rights holder about reuse. Permission for the club to display an image does not necessarily include permission for others to copy it.

Please link to the page containing an image rather than embedding an image served by this site in another website. This request concerns direct image embedding; it does not prohibit ordinary links to pages or publications.

## Fan fiction and contributions

The [fan-fiction archive](/fan-fiction/) contains contributions based on Mercedes Lackey’s works. A contributor’s rights in their own original material do not include ownership of underlying material belonging to other rights holders.

If a contribution carries a specific Creative Commons or other license, check its terms and scope. A contributor can grant only rights they own or are authorized to license; a license for their contribution does not automatically authorize use of underlying characters, settings, artwork, or other third-party material. A general reference to “Creative Commons” without identifying a license is not a clear permission grant.

Submitting material does not transfer its copyright to Queen’s Own. Publication arrangements should identify the rights the contributor grants to the club, attribution, any editing permission, and where the work may appear. Please confirm those arrangements with the editors before submitting work intended for publication. See the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/) for publication of personal information.

## Credits, permissions, and concerns

If you believe a credit is incorrect, material has been used without appropriate permission, or publication affects your rights, contact Linda A. Malcor at [shashtah@gmail.com](mailto:shashtah@gmail.com) or Kendra Hosseini at [healermoon@gmail.com](mailto:healermoon@gmail.com).

Please identify the material and its page or publication URL, explain your relationship to the work, and describe the correction or action requested. We will review the concern and, where appropriate, correct attribution, seek clarification or permission, or remove material. Adding a credit alone does not resolve a lack of permission.
