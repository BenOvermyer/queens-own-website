Call [Ed. note 2005:  Out-of-date info withheld.].
</p><p>
From Baen comes <i>Lammas Night</i>, created by Misty and edited by Josepha Sherman.  In Misty's song "Lammas Night," (on <i>Magic, Moondust &amp; Melancholy,</i> available from Firebird) a young woman is persuaded to become the weaver of spells for a small village.  As part of their persuasion, the villagers have given her the house of her predecessor.  She unwittingly partially frees his spirit and is courted by the half-seen phantom.  Is he her destinved lover, or something wicked that aims to entrap her?  Misty's song was purposely ambiguous, and left the ending up to the listener.  In this short story collection various writers present their endings.  <i>Lammas Night</i> contains stories by Misty, Josepha, Elisabeth Waters, Stephanie Shaver, Ru Emerson, Jody Lynn Nye, and more and is available in bookstores now or very soon.
</p><p>
Tanya Huff's <i>No Quarter</i> has an April release date and a beautiful Jody Lee cover.  This is a sequel to <i>Fifth Quarter</i>.  The cover quote is "They were the most feared pair in the Empire, and they were both trapped in the same body . . . "
</p><p>
There is no story by Misty in DAW's <i>Celebrity Vampires</i>, but there are a lot of enjoyable--but strange--tales for you vampire fans.  <i>Celebrity Vampires</i> is edited by Martin J. Greenberg.
</p><p>
Coming in April from DAW is the anthology <i>Warrior Enchantresses</i>, edited by Kathleen M. Massie-Ferch and Martin J. Greenberg.  Again, no story by Misty, but Melanie Rawn, Tanith Lee, Andre Norton, Jennifer Roberson, and other favorite authors are represented.

</p><h3>From Hearld Housemother:</h3>
<p>
Please, stomp reading the newsletter and find the envelope it came in.  Have I spelled your name correctly?  Is the address exactly right?  I temporarily lost two members because I switched numbers in their address.  Your regular mail carrier may know you and deliver the newsletter, but a substitute may try the wrong address and then give up.  If I get a newsletter back, I assume you've moved without a forwarding address and I remove your name from the mailing list.  Have I credited your dues payment?  I may have been interrupted while changing subscription dates and omitted you.  If writing about dues, tell me when you paid and how.  All checks and money orders are photocopied before being cashed.  All payments are recorded ina  book as well as in the computer.  Unless the cat hid your letter under the desk (regretfully, it has happened), I can trace your payment.  You can usually expect your newsletter wihtin a week of the mailing date.  If it doesn't arrive within two weeks, send me a postcard asking why.
</p><p>
Remember, if you want your mail delivered to you under your persona name, it's best to notify the post office.  You don't have to explain anything to them; just tell them that Goody Two-shoes or whoever will be getting her mail at your house, too.
</p><p>
Thank you to all who overpay your dues.  Your generosity allows me to respond to those who forget to include SASEs.
</p><p>
Please, please, PLEASE, type e-mail &amp; web page info!  I'll type in what I think you wrote, but it's hard enough deciphering postal codes and persona names.
</p><p>
I think all of us who attended <b>Queen's Own Collegia</b> at Dragon*Con last year had a wonderful time and planned to return. Unfortunately, I can not.  Morgan and her committee are working hard ot make this year's gathering a success.  I hope you'll give her your enthusiastic support.
