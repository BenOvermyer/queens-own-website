</p><p>
" . . . What can I say? I have an excellent wife; a lady of class and dignity, understood by few, most often seen by others in terms of her work, rather than her exceptional qualities of character.
</p><p>
"Who am I?
</p><p>
"Of myself, I can only say that I have had a full, adventurous, unlikely, rich, remarkable life, the details of which will likely never be even partially revealed. This is fine, really; there's more to a man that what's ever said or seen. I am the son of a great warrior, who is the son of a pilot/inventor and of a Lady, and my mother is an Oklahoma farm-girl who made good and travelled the world. I travelled Europe as a child with my folks, and had their support, as well as the faith and help of teachers and friends, in pursuing art, writing, and insight.
</p><p>
"I am a raptor rehabber, a volunteer firefighter, a sports car racer, an honored artisan, a respected sub-chief of my tribal band; I am a style maven, an architecture buff, a shopper, a music lover, an inventor, a behind-the-scenes influence; I am an advisor, an aviculturist, a stormspotter, a toy and automobile collector, a digital artist, a writer, a sharpshooter, a traveller, a skeptic.
</p><p>
"In aspect: a hawk, a gryphon, a fox, a bear, a dragon: I am imperfect but trying; flawed but repentant; most of a11---a human, nothing less.
</p><p>
"To all of you who I have been fortunate enough to meet and know, I thank you for influencing my growth and increasing my joy. "--Larry Dixon
