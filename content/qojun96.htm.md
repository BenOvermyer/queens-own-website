If I've missed anything, please let me know so I can list it in a future newsletter.
</p><p>
<i>Storm Breaking</i> is scheduled for an October release (I want it NOW, don't you?). <i>The Friends of Valdemar</i>, a short story collection, will be released this winter. The paperback version of <i>The Fire Rose</i> has a November release date. <i>Bardic Voices</i> I-III, a three-in-one trade paperback, has a February release date.
</p><p>
A subscription to <i>Marion Zimmer Bradley's FANTASY Magazine</i> [Ed. Note 2002:  Info withheld.  This magazine has ceased publication.]
</p><p>
The Spring catalog of Firebird Music is filled with treasures, including the emblem of the elite Silver Gryphon Guards. This stylized Gryphon design by Larry Dixon can be yours on a T-shirt, as a patch, or as a silver and black cloisonné pin. Firebird carries music for every taste from filk &amp; folk to the soundtracks from <i>Babylon 5</i> and <i>Alien Nation</i>; books by Josepha Sherman, Tanya Huff, Barbara Hambly and others; T-shirts, Celtic knot pewter pendants, videos of Steeleye Span and Tempest - 62 pages of treasure. And, of course, all of Misty's books and related music, art, jewelry, and stationary can be ordered from Firebird. Check out Firebird's most up-to date product information on the World Wide Web at [Ed. Note 2002:  Out-of-date info withheld.  Firebird is now located at <a href="http://www.firebirdarts.com/">http://www.firebirdarts.com/</a>].
</p><p>
This fall Baen is re-issuing <i>The Lark &amp; the Wren</i>, <i>Knight of Ghosts and Shadows</i> by Misty and Ellen Guon, and <i>Born to Run</i> by Misty and Larry, as well as the <i>Wing Commander</i> novel by Misty and Ellen, <i>Freedom Flight</i>, and the Misty/Piers Anthony collaboration, <i>If I Pay Thee Not in Gold</i>, all in paperback.
Also from Baen in November is Josepha Sherman's sequel to <i>The Shattered Oath</i>, <i>Forging the Runes</i>. Lois McMaster Bujold's <i>Shards of Honor</i> and <i>Barrayar</i> will be released in one volume as <i>Cordelia's Honor</i>. The new Miles Vorkosigan adventure, <i>Memorv</i>, has an October release date.

</p><h3>From Herald Housemother</h3>
<p>
To all who have been away at school or are going away fo rhte summer, remmeber to send me your address change.  Some colleges reurn mail rather than forwarding it.
</p><p>
I really appreciate typed addresses, especially e-mail addresses.  You know you live at 22S rather than 225, but I have to guess.  And e-mail?  I print 'em the way I see 'em, sometimes with disastrous results.
</p><p>
It seemed so simple, my plan to keep track of your subscritpion, but that was when the newsletter went out the second Saturday of the month, with no newsletter in April, August, or December.  Now I've decided to space the newsletters five or six weeks apart, and I've completley confused myself about when subscriptions end.  Projected mailing dates for the rest of '96 are 7/20. 8/24. 9/28, 11/2 and 12/7.  Prpposed dates for 1997 are 1/11, 2/22, 4/5, 5/17, 6/28, 8/2, 9/13, 10/25, and 12/6.  If the date on your mailing label doesn't seem right, check with me.  As I said, I'm confused; why shouldn't you be?
</p><p>
Thanks to all who sent me birthday greetings.  I'm feeling younger this year than last, probably because of an improvement in my health.  Life is good here in New Jersey.
</p><p><i>The Silver Gryphon</i> is available from the Science Fiction Book Club for just $8.98) plus shipping and handlings--at least $3 more).  If you would like membership information (6 free books, must buy 4 more within two years), send me a SASE.  IF you sent me a SASE in the past, heard nothing from me, and are still interested, just write and tell me.  SFBC and I had problems when I notified them of an address change, and they assured me my street didn't exist.  They have finally accepted my current address, so I am feeling kinder toward them.
