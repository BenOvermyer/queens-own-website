**Q:** How did this fan club originate?\
**A:** The idea was thought up during a posting on the Mercedes Lackey Mailing List . . . and was quickly dropped . . . However, one member thought it was a good enough idea to develop, so sought others to help develop it with him. Eventually the fan club began to form with one of members taking the possition of president . . . but after the initial ideas were planned and permission was secured from Mercedes Lackey . . . the president vanished . . . The boy was left with the whole club on his hands, and quickly sought someone else to head the club. Which he did, but that person too mysteriously vanished . . . So the undeveloped Vanyel Fan Club sat in its state of undeveloped existence for quite some time, living only as a "guestbook"-type file on the boy's system of Mercedes Lackey-related webpages, collecting names of those who were interested in joining such a club, but none of which were willing to take the possition of "president" . . . Until Bastian arrived . . .Within but a few short days, he lifted the weight of the club from the original conceptionist's shoulders and developed it, keeping true to its original creators' ideas . . .\
The club is now a chapter of [Queen's Own](/). We are still going strong and continues to develop.

**Q:** What is the Vanyel Fan Club?\
**A:** The Vanyel Fan Club is a club devoted to the fans of the main character from The Last Herald-Mage Trilogy by Mercedes Lackey.

**Q:** Who is the main character from Mercedes Lackey's The Last Herald-Mage Trilogy?\
**A:** Vanyel, of course . . .

**Q:** Who is Vanyel?\
**A:** You really *haven't* read the books, have you?\
Oh well . . .\
Herald-Mage Vanyel Ashkevron is the last Herald-Mage (a user of two very different types of magic) of Valdemar.

**Q:** Why a *Vanyel* fan club?\
**A:** Why not?

**Q:** What is it about Vanyel that you feel deserves a fan club all himself?\
**A:** Besides the fact that the character of Vanyel has revolutionized fantasy literature - that for the first time, there was a "shaych" character with whom teen "shaych" boys could relate, making it easier for them to accept their own "shaych-ness" - that he also has help "non-shaych" people become more accepting of real world "shaychness" - that in these profound ways with such a simple character, Mercedes Lackey has found a way to help ease a bit of bigotry . . . He's just a *really* cool character!

**Q:** Why do you call this an "official fan club"?\
**A:** Permission to have a Vanyel Fan Club has been secured from Mercedes Lackey.

**Q:** How do I join the Vanyel Fan Club?\
**A:** Merely by joining the [Vanyel Fan Club Chat List](/vanyel-fan-club/chat/). (We are an online-based organization, so this list is how we communicate with our members.)

**Q:** Is there a mailing list for this club?\
**A:** Yes! You can add yourself to thlist at [/vanyel-fan-club/chat/](/vanyel-fan-club/chat/)!

Q: Are there any club dues?\
A: No. The VFC is a chapter of Queen's Own. Membership is free.
