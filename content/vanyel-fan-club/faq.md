<b><span class="fs-plus2">Q:</span></b> How did this fan club originate?<br/>
<span class="fs-plus2"><b>A:</b></span> The idea was thought up during a posting on the Mercedes Lackey Mailing List . . . and was quickly dropped . . . However, one member thought it was a good enough idea to develop, so sought others to help develop it with him. Eventually the fan club began to form with one of members taking the possition of president . . . but after the initial ideas were planned and permission was secured from Mercedes Lackey . . . the president vanished . . . The boy was left with the whole club on his hands, and quickly sought someone else to head the club. Which he did, but that person too mysteriously vanished . . . So the undeveloped Vanyel Fan Club sat in its state of undeveloped existence for quite some time, living only as a "guestbook"-type file on the boy's system of Mercedes Lackey-related webpages, collecting names of those who were interested in joining such a club, but none of which were willing to take the possition of "president" . . . Until Bastian arrived . . .Within but a few short days, he lifted the weight of the club from the original conceptionist's shoulders and developed it, keeping true to its original creators' ideas . . .<br/>
The club is now a chapter of <a href="/">Queen's Own</a>.  We are still going strong and continues to develop.
</p><p>

<b><span class="fs-plus2">Q:</span></b> What is the Vanyel Fan Club?<br/>
<b><span class="fs-plus2">A:</span></b> The Vanyel Fan Club is a club devoted to the fans of the main character from The Last Herald-Mage Trilogy by Mercedes Lackey.
</p><p>
<b><span class="fs-plus2">Q:</span></b> Who is the main character from Mercedes Lackey's The Last Herald-Mage Trilogy?<br/>
<b><span class="fs-plus2">A:</span></b> Vanyel, of course . . .
</p><p>
<b><span class="fs-plus2">Q:</span></b> Who is Vanyel?<br/>
<b><span class="fs-plus2">A:</span></b> You really <i>haven't</i> read the books, have you?<br/>
Oh well . . .<br/>
Herald-Mage Vanyel Ashkevron is the last Herald-Mage (a user of two very different types of magic) of Valdemar.
</p><p>
<b><span class="fs-plus2">Q:</span></b> Why a <i>Vanyel</i> fan club?<br/>
<b><span class="fs-plus2">A:</span></b> Why not?
</p><p>
<b><span class="fs-plus2">Q:</span></b> What is it about Vanyel that you feel deserves a fan club all himself?<br/>
<b><span class="fs-plus2">A:</span></b> Besides the fact that the character of Vanyel has revolutionized fantasy literature - that for the first time, there was a "shaych" character with whom teen "shaych" boys could relate, making it easier for them to accept their own "shaych-ness" - that he also has help "non-shaych" people become more accepting of real world "shaychness" - that in these profound ways with such a simple character, Mercedes Lackey has found a way to help ease a bit of bigotry . . . He's just a <i>really</i> cool character!
</p><p>
<b><span class="fs-plus2">Q:</span></b> Why do you call this an "official fan club"?<br/>
<b><span class="fs-plus2">A:</span></b> Permission to have a Vanyel Fan Club has been secured from Mercedes Lackey.
</p><p>
