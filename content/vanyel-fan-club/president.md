Herald Bastian is the President of the official Vanyel Fan Club.\ A boy of only eighteen years, he decided that he couldn't bear to see the death of the fan club of a character from a trilogy of books that had meant so much to him . . .\ \ He became the Vanyel Fan Club President after offering to lift its weight from the shoulders of the one who had been left in charge of it after its prior presidents had each vanished . . .\ \ If you wish to know more about Herald Bastian, you may discover additional information at his personal website, [Bastian's Dreams](http://www.geocities.com/EnchantedForest/Meadow/6474) . . .\ \ \
