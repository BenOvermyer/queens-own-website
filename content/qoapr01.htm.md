Herald-Mage Danya has one more article to code for the next issue of <i>The Compass Rose</i>, and then it will be available online.  Sorry for the delay.  (In addition to having to bring a new computer system online, Danya has found out she�s pregnant again, so things have gotten a bit behind &lt;g&gt;.)
</p><p>
This month we�re going to take a brief hiatus from our artist interviews to talk about another type of art: costume design.  As several of our chapters gear up to attend special events, Renaissance Faires and conventions, we�ve been receiving questions yet again about how to find or make various Misty-oriented costumes.  We�ll start by discussing the most frequent requests: Herald�s Whites.
</p><p>
In <i>Arrow�s Flight</i>, Misty describes Talia�s whites as a raime shirt, leather breeches, and a leather tunic that pulled on over her head.  Everything was in white.  That�s the basic Herald�s uniform.
</p><p>
When Talia has to attend a formal court function in <i>Arrow�s Flight</i>, she wears an ankle-length gown of silk and velvet, slippers that tie to her ankles with ribbons, no train, detachable sleeves, and a silver ribbon in her hair.  It�s unclear whether the outfit is all in white, but presumably that is the case since Talia is on duty as Queen�s Own at the time, and all Heralds must wear all white when they are on duty.  Queen Selenay also wears a white shirt and skirt, with either a white tunic or vest, when she holds court; her materials are usually velvet and silk as well.
</p><p>
Cloaks vary from horseman�s type to full-length with hood.  The important factor is that the cloaks are completely white.
</p><p>
In <i>Winds of Change</i> Elspeth gives instructions to the hertasi who want to improve the look of her whites.  Essentially, she tells them that the clothing must be nonreflective, silent (no bells or anything else that will make noise), tailored (at least to the point that nothing will catch on anything while she�s fighting or riding), and, above all, white.  No off-white, cream, eggshell or anything else is acceptable.  The hertasi come back with something that makes her look something like a female version of Buffalo Bill:  cut-out leather vest with fringe, cut-out leather trousers, silk shirt, and both half boots and full boots.  Everything is in white, as per Elspeth�s instructions.
</p><p>
When Elspeth gets back to Haven in <i>Winds of Fury</i>, the Heralds in the Circle see her new whites and start lusting after them &lt;g&gt;.  The result is that Herald�s Whites are still available in the "uniform" style that Talia wore but individualized versions, such as Elspeth�s, start cropping up, particularly among the new Herald-Mages.
</p><p>
So on our alternate timeline, people have quite a bit of latitude when it comes to creating Whites.  Stick to Elspeth�s rules:
</p><p>
</p><ul>
<li>	All white.
</li><li>	Nothing shiny.
</li><li>	Nothing that makes noise.
</li><li>	Close-fitting so as not to impede riding or fighting.
</li></ul>
<p>
Medieval and Renaissance patterns are recommended.  Simplicity pattern 8587 A (vest-you might want to exclude the short sleeves), B (shirt with slit at neck), C (trousers) works well.  Simplicity pattern 9452 offers several options for a cloak/cape.  Remember that there are no buttons or zippers or Velcro mentioned; everything fastens with ties, laces or other such devices.  Muslin or linen would work well for the shirt, although you could certainly go to silk if you were so inclined.  If you can�t afford leather or suede for the trousers and vest, there are a variety of fabrics available in white.  Steer clear of things like polyester.  Cotton, linen, silk and other such natural fabrics are preferred.  Artists� renditions of the boots vary, but since the Heralds use stirrups, some sort of heel would be advisable, even though that isn�t strictly true to medieval style.  Either half boots or full boots (below the knee) would be acceptable.  (There is nothing to suggest that Heralds use the sort of hip-high or thigh-high cavalry boots that show up in the Renaissance.  Also, most Renaissance Faires prefer it if you keep leather fringe off your boots.)  You can decorate your Whites with embroidery, beading and so forth to your heart�s content as long as you don�t jingle when you walk &lt;g&gt;.  
</p><p>
We�ll take a look at some other costume design ideas in future issues of this newsletter as space permits.
</p><p>
