Dear Misty Fans,
</p><p>
<img align="right" alt="Border Art (image unavailable in archive)" border="0" data-unavailable-target="https://queensown.org/qo267.htm" loading="lazy"/>
WOW! A new Misty book to read--<i>Storm Breaking</i>--and unexpected good news. Misty and Larry will attend Dragon*Con in Atlanta next June. We'll tell you more about it in future newsletters; this is just advance notice so you can start planning to attend too.  <i>Storm Breaking</i> is a hardback, with interior illustrations by Larry, and more than a few surprises. (That's what's on the other side of the Iftel border? I never guessed!)
</p><p>
News flash from Firebird Arts &amp; Music: Mark Shepherd is doing an instrumental album to go along with his newest book. The working title of both is <i>Spirit Ride</i> and the story is set in the same universe as <i>Elvendude</i>. The release dates for book and album are unscheduled. Misty and Paul Espinoza of Golden Bough
are writing songs for a <i>Firebird</i> album. No, not the story of our favorite recording company, but Misty's retelling of the Russian Firebird legend. The book, a hardback, has a December release date and the album should be ready in February.
</p><p>
The Firebird Music Special Summer Update catalog has T-shirts for every taste, from beautiful Celtic and Michael Whelan designs to the bizarre and strange. (Imagine "Curiosity Kilt the Cat" and "Feline Groovy.") Of course there's music: TEMPEST, various Celtic artists, Michael Longcor, native American music by R.
Carlos Nakai and others, and David Arkenstone. You can order all of Misty's books from Firebird as well as an <i>Arrows of the Queen</i> Concordance or songbook, <i>Silver Gryphon</i> patches and pins, related jewelry and stationery, and more. Soon to come is a <i>Shadow Stalker</i> Songbook.
</p><p>
Check out the Firebird on-line catalog at [Ed. note 2005:  Out-of-date info withheld.]  for the most up to date product information. E-mail orders may be sent to [Ed. note 2005:  Out-of-date info withheld.]. To reach Firebird by phone use [Ed. note 2005:  Out-of-date info withheld.]. Firebird's address is [Ed. note 2005:  Out-of-date info withheld.].
</p><p>
TEMPEST will tour the Southwest in November. For booking and information call [Ed. note 2005:  Out-of-date info withheld.]. You can e-mail TEMPEST at [Ed. note 2005:  Out-of-date info withheld.].
</p><p>
Current Westcoast dates are: Saturday, October 5 - Placerville Music Festival Nugget Campground [Ed. note 2005:  Out-of-date info withheld.]; Saturday, October 12 - Monterey Carleton Hall [Ed. note 2005:  Out-of-date info withheld.]; Friday, November 22 - Fresno Club Fred [Ed. note 2005:  Out-of-date info withheld.]; Saturday, November 23 - Modesto State Theatre [Ed. note 2005:  Out-of-date info withheld.].
</p><p>
This fall Baen is re-issuing <i>The Lark &amp; the Wren, Knight of Ghosts and Shadows</i> by Misty and Ellen Guon, and <i>Bom to Run</i>, by Misty and Larry, as well as the Wing Commander novel by Misty and Ellen, <i>Freedonz Flight</i>, and the Misty/Piers Anthony collaboration, <i>If I Pay Thee Not in Gold</i>, all in paperback.
</p><p>
Also from Baen in November is Josepha Sherman's sequel to <i>The Shattered Oath, Forging the Runes</i>. Lois McMaster Bujold's <i>Shards of Honor</i> and <i>Barrayar</i> will be released in one volume as <i>Cordelia's Honor</i>. The new Miles Vorkosigan adventure, <i>Memory</i>, is in bookstores now.
