From Misty:
</p><p>
Well, 'tis the season, and I've been working my patootie off to see that 
everyone gets a high quota of Misty prose for the coming year. Finally, 
after far too long following the disastrous fire in Larry's studio, the 
lawsuit with our insurance company, and oh, yes, the nutcase who thought 
Guardians were real, I feel like I'm back on my form, and so does Larry. I 
hope this is showing in what we're doing.
</p><p>
By the end of the year, I expect to have two more collaborations out the 
door, <i>MAD MAUDLIN</i> in the Bedlam Bards series with Rosemary Edgehill, and the 
first book of an entirely new series, a prequel to SERRAted Edge, that we're 
calling <i>Doubled Edge</i>, with famed romance writer Roberta Gellis. This book 
is called <i>THIS SCEPTER'D ISLE</i> and is about Keighvin Silverhair's cousins and 
Henry Fitzroy, Henry VIII's illegitimate son. On the boards for publication 
in April is the start of a new series, <i>JOUST</i>, the first chapter of which 
should be on the Mercedes Lackey site. I'm also collaborating on another 
new high fantasy trilogy with James Mallory, the author of the "Merlin" 
books based on the TV series---the first of which, <i>THE MOUNTAIN'S SHADOW</i>, is 
a whopper, coming in at well over 250,000 words. Whew! The book has been 
turned in to TOR, so stay in touch to see when the publication date is.
And of course, you'll be getting your second half of Alberich's story, 
<i>EXILE'S VALOR</i>, and the second Lackey/Flint/Freer, <i>THIS ROUGH MAGIC</i>.
</p><p>
Things are very exciting for Larry, too; he has been chosen as the artist to 
design and illustrate the GURPS roleplaying handbook for Lois Bujold's 
Vorkosiverse, giving a coherent vision to the entire project. He'll be 
doing more covers for Baen books, including a new cover for <i>YOUNG MILES</i>, and 
several covers and interiors for various roleplaying magazines. He's also 
coming in on the SERRAted Edge book, <i>STONED SOULS</i>.
</p><p>
Altogether a lot of exciting and energizing projects coming up for both of 
us individually and together.
</p><p>
I hope that the coming year brings good things for all of us. Happy 
Holidays to you all.
</p><p>
