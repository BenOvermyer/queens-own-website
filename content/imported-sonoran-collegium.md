</p><p>
</p><ul>
<li>A strong community based on our love of Mercedes Lackey's works
</li><li>Regional field trips!  (Hopefully including some
camping opportunities, conventions, Renaissance
faires, concerts, any excuse to gather &lt;g&gt;.)
</li><li>A monthly e-newsletter (that will eventually become . . .
</li><li>A hardcopy newsletter with fan art, and fan fic
</li><li>Discussions and group events that emphasize the
importance of Heralds (i.e., community involvement and
volunteer work!)
</li><li>Support of the arts (with a strong emphasis on
enjoyment of the same)
</li><li>Wouldn't it be cool to eventually see if we could
get together a filk group? Maybe have our own
gathering just to jam and share music?!
</li></ul>
